# Plans and entitlements

## What it does

Every tenant is on one plan, `FREE`, `STARTER` or `GROWTH`, which an operator sets. The plan is a ceiling: it decides which reader-facing capabilities the site may switch on, how many languages it serves, and which tenant pages the owner sees in the platform. Inside that ceiling the owner switches each capability on or off on the Features page, and the site shows a capability only when both the plan and the toggle allow it. A site created through self-serve onboarding starts on `GROWTH` in status `TRIAL`, with `trial_ends_at` 14 days (`TRIAL_LENGTH_DAYS`) after creation and no Stripe subscription. There is no billing or self-serve plan change yet; otherwise the plan is a column an operator edits. A trial that ends without a subscription drops to `FREE`, and any site that leaves a paid plan or a trial for `FREE` gets a 14-day downgrade grace before its extra team members lock and its custom domain stops being the main address.

## Surfaces and routes

- **Platform, operator** — the plan picker in the Add tenant form and the tenant details panel; the plan shows in the tenants table and on the owner's home card.
- **Owner email** — a saved plan change emails the owner once (see Configuration). The downgrade grace emails the owner on day 0, 7 and 13 (see Constraints and invariants).
- **Platform, Features page** — `/dashboard/features` (owner) and `/tenants/{id}/features` (operator). Six capability rows: Reader accounts is the main switch, with Bookmarks indented under it and disabled while it is off. Comments and Ratings have no row on any plan, for the owner or the operator (`SHOWN_CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`): ratings are dropped and comments are hidden until built. A "Coming soon" row has no switch, and a row outside the plan is locked with a badge naming the cheapest plan that holds it (`lowestPlanWithCapability`): "Starter plan" for the contact form on Free, "Growth plan" for the rest. While Reader accounts is off, a note says nobody new can sign in.
- **Platform, plan-gated pages** — `/dashboard/languages` and `/dashboard/email` answer 404 when the plan does not open them. Subscribers and Team are sidebar entries with a "Coming soon" badge and no page; the sidebar has no Comments entry.
- **Platform, Sign-in page** — `/dashboard/sign-in` (owner) and `/tenants/{id}/sign-in` (operator, read-only), right after Domain in the Configuration menu on every plan. One card per provider (Google, then GitHub) with its status pill ("Shared app" or "Your own app") and the exact callback address, `https://<main address>/api/auth/callback/<provider>`, with Copy. A Growth owner adds, replaces and removes a client ID and secret per provider; a Free or Starter site's owner sees the Growth note above two locked rows, whose "See what's included" lists your own Google app and your own GitHub app as plain items and whose "Ask about Growth" opens the Upgrade page ([billing](./billing.md)); the operator view shows no note.
- **Public site** — no route of its own. It gates `/bookmarks` (404 unless Reader accounts and Bookmarks are both on) and `/account` (404 there only to a signed-out visitor on a site with Reader accounts off), the header account menu, the bookmark button, the newsletter module and its server action, Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights, and the consent banner. A page renders only the module types its tenant's plan entitles.
- **Studio** — a module type whose capability is off is relabelled "(off in Features)" in pickers, and its documents carry a warning; `module_newsletter` and `module_contactForm` map to a capability. Given module-pack entitlements, a module type outside them shows as unavailable in a page's module picker: every plan's "Add item…" menu is the same picker — search, the All and kind tabs, the same rows, icons and order, each row titled in bold with its module's own title — with the module disabled in its usual place and a badge naming its pack's plan ("Starter" for the contact form, "Growth" for the rest) at the right of its row, while the per-row "Add item before/after" menus (Sanity's own, which can't be replaced) list it last with a lock and a "— Starter plan" or "— Growth plan" title. It offers no existing document to pick there, and can't be created new from the desk, the global Create menu or a reference's "Create new"; its existing documents stay listed and editable, with a banner, and a page referencing one shows a warning on that item that never blocks publishing. When a type is both off-plan and off in Features, the plan message shows.

## Data

- `tenants.plan` — required, the `tenant_plan` pgEnum built from `TENANT_PLAN`.
- `PLAN_REGISTRY` (`@blog/db/constants`) — `FREE`: `CONSENT_BANNER`. `STARTER`: `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM`. `GROWTH`: all eight `CAPABILITY` keys (`READER_ACCOUNTS`, `COMMENTS`, `RATINGS`, `BOOKMARKS`, `NEWSLETTER`, `ANALYTICS`, `CONSENT_BANNER`, `CONTACT_FORM`). Ratings is Growth only because rating needs sign-in.
- `PLAN_LOCALE_LIMIT` — languages per plan including the default: `FREE` 1, `STARTER` 1, `GROWTH` 3.
- `PLAN_MONTHLY_SENDS_PER_SUBSCRIBER` and `SUBSCRIBER_BAND_CEILINGS` (1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000) — the monthly newsletter allowance: `FREE` 0, `STARTER` 0, `GROWTH` 6 × the ceiling of the band the active-subscriber count falls in, or 6 × the count above 25,000. `monthlySendAllowance(plan, activeSubscriberCount)` applies it.
- `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS` (`@blog/db/constants`) — one record per plan. `lookFields` lists the `site_config` Look columns (`LOOK_FIELDS`) the plan may set: `FREE` and `STARTER` the preset, accent hue, logo, favicon and Cookie settings button; `GROWTH` every Look column. `modulePacks` lists the module packs the plan entitles: `FREE` `CORE`; `STARTER` `CORE` and `STARTER`; `GROWTH` all three. `hasPoweredByMark` says whether the site's footer carries the "Powered by" mark: `FREE` yes, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` no (see [footer-and-legal-pages](./footer-and-legal-pages.md)). `hasCustomDomain` says whether the owner may connect their own domain and make it the main address: `FREE` no, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` yes; the Domain page's card and the connect, verify and make-main actions read it, and none compares against a plan name. `hasOwnOAuthApps` says whether the site signs readers in through its own Google and GitHub apps when it has saved credentials for them: `FREE` and `STARTER` no, `GROWTH` yes (see [reader-auth](./reader-auth.md)). `hasOwnAnalytics` says whether the site loads the owner's own analytics tool: `FREE` no, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` yes; `getTenantAnalytics` reads it (see [consent-and-analytics](./consent-and-analytics.md)). `seats` is how many people the site may have in its team, counting the owner, every membership and every pending invite: `FREE` 1, `STARTER` 1, `GROWTH` 3. The epic's later entitlements join this record as fields of their own.
- `tenant_oauth_credentials` — one row per tenant and provider: `client_id`, the secret encrypted under `TENANT_OAUTH_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, and nullable `callback_domain`, the main address the owner last registered (null reads as the current one). Saving a card, or "I've added it", sets it to the current main address.
- `tenant_analytics` — at most one row per tenant (`tenant_id` primary key, cascade delete): `provider` (`ANALYTICS_PROVIDER`) and `analytics_id`, both `NOT NULL`; no row means no analytics.
- `settings_features` — one row per tenant (unique `tenant_id`, cascade delete), eight `NOT NULL` boolean toggles. Provisioning writes no row; an absent row resolves to the tenant's current preset's `featureDefaults` (both presets: bookmarks and contact form on; reader accounts, comments, ratings, newsletter, analytics and consent banner off). The column defaults match.
- `MODULE_PACK` (`CORE`, `STARTER`, `GROWTH`) and `MODULE_PACK_BY_TYPE` (`@blog/config/constants`) give every module `_type` exactly one pack; the map `satisfies Record<TModuleType, TModulePack>`, so a new module type fails type-check until it has one. Core: the three heroes, the five Posts modules, content, FAQ, embed, section pages, CTA, newsletter and cookie settings. Starter: contact form. Growth: testimonial, team, logo wall, stats, pricing, feature list, feature highlights and timeline. `isModuleTypeEntitled(type, packs)` is the one check; an unknown type is never entitled.
- `SELLABLE_TENANT_PLANS` (`@blog/config/constants`) — the plans sold through Stripe, today `STARTER` and `GROWTH`; `@blog/studio` reads it, so the Studio names no plan. `PLAN_PRICE_LOOKUP_KEYS` is typed so a sellable plan must have lookup keys and every other plan must be `null`; the two cannot drift.
- `PLAN_PRICE_LOOKUP_KEYS` (`@blog/db/constants`) — each plan's Stripe Price lookup keys, never Price ids or amounts: `FREE` none; `STARTER` `STARTER_MONTHLY` and `STARTER_YEARLY`, with no band keys; `GROWTH` `GROWTH_MONTHLY`, `GROWTH_YEARLY` and, per paid subscriber band above the included 1,000, a monthly and a yearly key: `GROWTH_BAND_2500` and `GROWTH_BAND_2500_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_5000` and `GROWTH_BAND_5000_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_10000` and `GROWTH_BAND_10000_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_25000` and `GROWTH_BAND_25000_YEARLY`. These twelve strings are the lookup keys set on the Prices in Stripe. The band keys sit in a full record over the paid band ceilings of `SUBSCRIBER_BAND_CEILINGS`, so a new ceiling fails type-check until it has both keys. Every item on one Stripe subscription bills on one interval, so a yearly Growth subscription carries the yearly band Price; a yearly band costs ten times the monthly one. A key names the same Price in Stripe's test and live modes, and each Price carries dollars, euros and pounds as currency options; the amounts live only in Stripe, and a price change moves the key to a new Price with no deploy. The record is `Record<TTenantPlan, …>`, so a new plan fails type-check until it has its keys. The Stripe webhook reads it to map a subscription back to a plan and band ([billing](./billing.md)).
- Billing state on `tenants`, all nullable: `stripe_customer_id` and `stripe_subscription_id` (each unique), `stripe_promotion_code` (the code the subscription started with, for campaign attribution), `subscriber_band_ceiling` (the paid subscriber band the Stripe subscription carries, 2,500 to 25,000, read from its band Price; null means the included 1,000), `trial_ends_at`, `downgrade_grace_started_at`, and one sent-at stamp per grace email (`grace_started_email_sent_at`, `grace_halfway_email_sent_at`, `grace_ending_email_sent_at`). `getTenantBilling` and `setTenantBilling` read and write them; in a write, a field left out keeps its value and `null` clears it. `setTenantPlan(tenantId, { plan, status })` is the plan-change write the webhook uses. It writes only an `ACTIVE`, `TRIAL` or `PAST_DUE` tenant (`WRITABLE_TENANT_STATUSES`) and returns a `TENANT_PLAN_WRITE_OUTCOME`: `WRITTEN` with the stored plan and status, `NOT_WRITABLE` with the current status for an archived or suspended tenant, which it leaves unchanged, or `NOT_FOUND`; it and `updateTenantDetails` clear `self_serve_owner_email_index` in the same statement on any move to a paid plan ([platform sign-in and onboarding](./platform-sign-in-and-onboarding.md)). `getTenantIdByStripeIds({ stripeCustomerId?, stripeSubscriptionId? })` returns a `STRIPE_TENANT_LOOKUP_OUTCOME`: `FOUND` with the `tenantId` of the tenant carrying either id, so a tenant whose subscription id is not stored yet is still found; `CONFLICT` with `customerTenantId` and `subscriptionTenantId` when the two ids name different tenants, which the caller logs and writes nothing for; `NOT_FOUND` when no tenant carries either id, or when neither id is given (an empty string counts as not given) without querying.
- Downgrade grace: `planChangeFields(plan, status, now)` sets `downgrade_grace_started_at` to now, records the plan the site left in `downgraded_from_plan`, and clears the three email stamps, when a site leaves a paid plan or a trial for `FREE` (its current plan is not `FREE`, or its status is `TRIAL`); a Free site written `FREE` again keeps its grace, and a move to a paid plan clears the grace, its stamps and `downgraded_from_plan`. `updateTenantDetails` and `setTenantPlan` both write through it, so an operator plan change, the Stripe webhook and the trial end all start or end the grace the same way, and the same statement unlocks the locked memberships that fit the new plan's seats (`unlockMembershipsWithinPlan`; `setTenantPlan` only when it writes the plan). `endTenantTrial(tenantId, now)` moves a `TRIAL` tenant past `trial_ends_at` with no `stripe_subscription_id` and not archived to `FREE` + `ACTIVE`, keeping its `self_serve_owner_email_index` (a trial and a Free site share the slot), and returns the row only to the caller that ended it; `listTenantsWithEndedTrial(now)` lists the same tenants. Grace days are counted in whole UTC days from the day the grace started, so the time a sweep runs never moves a step to another day. `GRACE_EMAIL` (`STARTED`, `HALFWAY`, `ENDING`) is due from `GRACE_EMAIL_DAY` (0, 7, 13) until the next email's day, the last until `DOWNGRADE_GRACE_DAYS` (14); `listTenantsDueForGraceEmail(email, now)` lists the tenants whose email is due and unstamped, and `claimGraceEmail(tenantId, email, now)` stamps it in one conditional update, returning the row only to the caller that claimed it. `listTenantsDueForGraceEnd(now)` lists the tenants on day 14 or later, and `endDowngradeGrace(tenantId, graceStartedAt)` clears the grace, its stamps and `downgraded_from_plan` only while the stored start still matches and the plan is `FREE`, returning the row only to the caller that ended it.
- `memberships.locked_at` — nullable. Every day-14 write — the lock, stopping invites, `setTenantPrimaryDomain` and `deleteCustomDomainClaim` given a plan — applies only while the tenant is still on that plan, checked in the same statement (`isTenantOnPlan`), so a payment that lands mid-sweep is never undone; a failed operator details save re-locks only the members its unlock released, never one the grace left unlocked. A locked membership is kept, never deleted, and is invisible to `getMembership` and `listMembershipsWithTenantsForUser`, so its member cannot reach the site's admin. Seats rank the owner first and the rest by when they joined. `lockMembershipsOverSeats(tenantId, plan, now)` locks the unlocked memberships past the plan's seats and returns how many it locked; `countMembershipsOverSeats(tenantId, seats)` counts them without locking. `stopPendingInvitesOverSeats(tenantId, plan)` deletes the unaccepted non-owner invites, latest first, that no longer fit beside the unlocked memberships. `consumeMembershipInvite` claims a non-owner invite only while the tenant's unlocked memberships are under its plan's seats, leaving it pending otherwise, and after the insert locks the member who joined last if two accepts raced past the seats.
- `stripe_webhook_events` — one row per handled Stripe event id (`event_id` primary key, `handled_at`). `recordStripeEvent(eventId)` returns `RECORDED` the first time and `ALREADY_HANDLED` for a redelivered or replayed id, so of two concurrent deliveries exactly one is recorded. Recording marks the event handled, so the webhook records it only after its own writes succeed; a failed handler then leaves the id unrecorded and Stripe's retry is processed.
- **Caching (web)** — `getTenantPlan` and `getEffectiveSettingsFeatures` are `unstable_cache` per tenant, tagged `tenant-plan:<id>` and `settings-features:<id>`, with `TENANT_CONFIG_REVALIDATE_SECONDS` (3600) as the fallback. `POST /api/revalidate-site-config` expires both tags with `site-config:<id>` and purges the whole layout. The platform and Studio read `tenants.plan` uncached on every request.

## Configuration

- **Operator** (any `admins` row) sets the plan through `createTenantAction` and `updateTenantDetailsAction`, both behind `requireAdmin`. The plan stays editable in the tenant details panel once provisioning has succeeded, and locks only while provisioning is running and on an archived tenant. A change applies on the site's next page load and deletes no stored data; a downgrade only hides what the new plan does not hold.
- **Plan change email** — when `updateTenantDetailsAction` saves a plan that differs from the stored one, or the Stripe webhook moves a site to another plan ([billing](./billing.md)), its caller runs `onTenantPlanChange(tenantId, fromPlan, toPlan)` — the one step every plan change runs — in `after()`, once the response has gone; it calls `notifyOwnerOfPlanChange`, which emails the owner through `@blog/email`'s `buildOwnerPlanChangeEmail`: the notice for the new plan, worded to read right whichever plan it came from, with an "Open your dashboard" button to the tenant's dashboard. The copy is fixed platform copy (never Voice), in the `ownerPlanChangeEmail` namespace of the platform's message catalogues, written in the site's default language (`tenants.locale`). It goes to `getTenantOwnerEmail`, which is the pending owner-invite address until the owner joins, from `OWNER_EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`. With no sender address or `PLATFORM_APP_BASE_URL` it is skipped and logged (`tenants.plan_change_email_unconfigured`); with no owner address too (`tenants.plan_change_email_owner_missing`). A failed send is logged (`tenants.plan_change_email_failed`) and never fails or undoes the save.
- **Owner or operator** saves Features through `updateFeaturesAction`, behind `requireTenantMembership` (any member; an operator gets a virtual owner membership).
- **Coming soon** — `COMMENTS`, `RATINGS` and `NEWSLETTER` carry `isComingSoon` in `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`, and the save writes them off whatever the payload says. Only `NEWSLETTER` is shown, with a "Coming soon" badge and no switch; `COMMENTS` and `RATINGS` are left out of `SHOWN_CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`, and their stored toggles and columns stay. An owner can switch `READER_ACCOUNTS`, `BOOKMARKS`, `ANALYTICS`, `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM` on `GROWTH`, `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM` on `STARTER`, and only `CONSENT_BANNER` on `FREE`.
- **Reader accounts** — `BOOKMARKS`, `COMMENTS` and `RATINGS` carry `parentField: 'readerAccountsEnabled'` in `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`. While the parent is off, a sub-item can be saved off but never on (`blockSwitchOnWhileParentOff`), so its stored value comes back when the parent is switched on again, and `getEnabledCapabilities` reports it off (`withParentOffApplied`). Look's account options and the public site's sign-in both follow `READER_ACCOUNTS`, and on the public site a sub-item applies only while it is on (`isReaderAccountFeatureEnabled`).
- **Plan page access** (`planPageAccess`) — Languages when `PLAN_LOCALE_LIMIT` > 1; Email when the plan holds bookmarks, comments, newsletter or contact form; Subscribers with newsletter; Team when the plan's `seats` is more than 1 (`GROWTH` only today). No page gate names a plan. The owner's sidebar omits the rest and their pages 404; the operator's `/tenants/{id}` view shows every entry and page whatever the plan.
- **Look** — fields outside `lookFields` show locked on the Look page with a Growth badge beside the label, at the preset's value, focusable and announced as unavailable (see [look](./look.md)). On a plan with locked fields (`FREE` and `STARTER`) the owner also sees the Growth note, and the reset-to-preset action is hidden; the note's "See what's included" lists what Growth adds on the page.
- **Module packs in Studio** — `StudioMount` takes an optional plain-data `entitlements` object whose `modulePacks` lists the entitled packs. Omitted, every pack is entitled: the site is the enforcement point, and the Sanity CLI Studio has no tenant. The platform's `StudioMountView` passes the tenant plan's `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS` `modulePacks`, read on every load, so the owner's `/dashboard` Studio and the operator's `/tenants/{id}` Studio both follow the current plan.
- **Module packs on the site** — `renderModules` wraps each module in `ModuleGate`, and `renderHeroModule` runs the same check: `isModuleEntitled` reads `getRequestContext().tenantId` and the cached `getTenantPlan`, once per request, and renders a module only when `isModuleTypeEntitled` passes for the plan's `modulePacks`. A skipped module renders nothing, and its component never runs, so its data is never fetched. With no tenant (the local and dev platform fallback) every module renders. A downgrade deletes no content; an upgrade shows the modules again once the plan cache refreshes.
- **Powered-by mark** — `SiteFooter` draws `Footer.PoweredBy` when `getPoweredByMark` finds the plan's `hasPoweredByMark` set. It reads the plan through `getEffectivePlan`, the cached `getTenantPlan` with Free's rules when the plan cannot be read (`site_config.plan_fetch_failed`), the same reader `getSiteConfig` uses for the Look. No call site compares against `FREE`.
- **Own sign-in apps** — `saveSignInAppAction`, `removeSignInAppAction` and `confirmSignInCallbackAction` (`apps/platform/src/server/sign-in-apps/`) run behind `requireSignInAppOwner` (the site's real owner only) and refuse an archived site; the save also refuses a plan without `hasOwnOAuthApps`. A save without a secret changes only the client ID of a saved app and keeps the stored secret. Save and remove revalidate the site and record `OAUTH_APP_SAVED`, `OAUTH_APP_REPLACED` or `OAUTH_APP_REMOVED` with the provider, never a credential. A saved secret never leaves the server: the page shows it as "Set". The full client ID reaches only the owner of an unarchived site, masked to its first six characters until Show; members and archived sites get the masked value, and the operator view none. After the main address changes, each own-app card warns until the owner saves it or confirms, and the Domain page's main-address confirmation names the providers with a saved app. A downgrade keeps the saved apps and shows "Your saved app is kept"; a kept app counts as the shared app everywhere, the operator view included, until an upgrade. Leaving the page with unsaved typing in either card asks first, counting the unsaved fields across both cards.
- **Not plan-gated** — Voice, Domain, Studio, the Features page itself, page types, AI takeaways.

## Constraints and invariants

- Most restrictive wins: `isCapabilityEnabled` is `PLAN_REGISTRY[plan].includes(capability) && toggle`. Every failure path returns `false`. A fetch error also logs (`settings_features.plan_fetch_failed` or `effective_fetch_failed`); a missing tenant or plan does not. A render site omits the capability silently, never with a placeholder.
- Modules on the site: when the plan can't be read, only `CORE` modules render and `module_renderer.plan_fetch_failed` is logged; a missing plan also falls back to `CORE` without a log. Nothing in the module path reads request headers, so ISR pages stay static.
- The server re-checks entitlement on every plan-gated save, and a disabled control is never the gate. Look (`updateLookAction`), Features (`updateFeaturesAction`) and Languages (`updateTenantLanguagesAction`) refuse a save only when it changes something the current plan does not include: a value different from both the stored one and the preset value the plan shows instead. An out-of-plan value still as stored never blocks a save, so a page opened before a downgrade can still save its allowed fields. A refused save writes nothing and returns one shape, `toNotInPlanRefusal` (`apps/platform/src/utils/plan-refusal/`): `REFUSED` with `SETTINGS_REFUSAL.NOT_IN_PLAN`, the tenant's current `plan` and the refused fields or languages. A later plan-gated action adopts it by returning that helper. Features logs the refusal as `settings_features.plan_entitlement_rejected`, Languages as its `UPDATE_LANGUAGES_REJECTED` event.
- The platform tells the owner at save time and nowhere else, with no polling or refresh on focus. `useSettingsForm` handles a `NOT_IN_PLAN` refusal for every settings page: it shows "Your site is on the {plan} plan, which doesn't include {items}, so nothing was saved." (`saveBar.notInPlan`), labelling each item from the page's draft fields, and puts the refused items back to their saved values while keeping every other edit. When the refusal's plan differs from the one `TenantPlanProvider` (`useTenantPlan`, mounted in the owner's and the operator's tenant layouts) rendered the page with, it calls `router.refresh()` once, so every lock on the page updates together.
- A downgrade deletes nothing. Toggles are clamped on read: on the site, in Studio (`getEnabledCapabilities`) and in the Features page's initial values. Saving that page never writes an out-of-plan toggle: `updateFeaturesAction` keeps its stored value, so an upgrade brings back what was on. Switching on one that is stored off is refused.
- Look: `toEffectiveLook` (`@blog/db/utils/effective-look/effective-look`, client-safe) gives each field outside the plan its preset's value, in the platform's form and preview and in `apps/web`'s `getSiteConfig`; `updateLookAction` refuses a change to a field outside the plan (`listRefusedLookFields`) and otherwise writes only the fields the plan includes (`pickEntitledLook`), so a downgrade keeps the stored choices and an upgrade shows them again.
- Languages: the live set is the default plus the first `limit − 1` stored additional languages. `setTenantAdditionalLocales` refuses to add a locale past the limit (`DB_LOCALE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) but lets a downgraded tenant keep or reorder its stored ones.
- Seats: `createMembershipInvite` refuses a new invite with the `MEMBERSHIP_INVITE_OUTCOME.SEAT_LIMIT` outcome, writing nothing, once the tenant's memberships plus pending invites reach the plan's `seats`. Two invites sent at once can both pass that check, so each re-counts the tenant's memberships plus pending invites in a fresh statement after inserting; while the count is over the plan's `seats`, the invite deletes itself and returns `SEAT_LIMIT`, whatever its rank. The tenant never ends over its limit: if two racing invites both see the overflow, both are refused and the owner retries. A caller sends the invite email only for a `CREATED` result. A re-invite of an address already invited reports that invite, even at the limit. Seating the owner at tenant creation, or through an owner invite, never checks the limit, because the owner is always within it. Accepting any other invite (`consumeMembershipInvite`) takes a seat only while the tenant's unlocked memberships are under its plan's seats, and leaves the invite pending otherwise. A downgrade deletes no membership: it refuses new invites until the tenant is under the limit, and when its grace ends it locks the memberships over the limit and stops the unaccepted invites that no longer fit.
- **Downgrade grace (worker).** The daily cron (`GET /api/inactivity-sweep`) runs `runDowngradeGraceSweep` after the inactivity stages, skipping the platform tenant, in this order: it ends every unpaid trial past its end (`endTenantTrial`) and purges that site's cache; it ends every grace 14 days old; then it sends the day-13, day-7 and day-0 emails, latest first, so a trial ended in this sweep gets its day-0 email in the same run and one sweep never sends two. Each email is claimed with `claimGraceEmail` before it is sent, so a second sweep the same day sends nothing twice. What will lock at day 14 is decided when each email is due, from the current plan's `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS`: the memberships over its `seats` (`countMembershipsOverSeats`) and, on a plan without `hasCustomDomain`, a `primaryDomain` outside `TENANT_ROOT_DOMAIN`. The day-0 email is always sent: the site is on Free, what stopped (from Growth or a trial: Growth sections, the newsletter, reader accounts and the extra Look settings; from Starter: the contact form and the site's own analytics tool, with the "Powered by" mark back), nothing is deleted, and how to upgrade; when something will lock it also names it and the date. The day-7 and day-13 emails go only when something will lock, are neither claimed nor sent otherwise, and name what will lock (decided, owner, 2026-11-13). Their copy is fixed, in the site's default language (`OWNER_GRACE_EMAIL_COPY`), with the grace's end date written in that language and a button to the admin dashboard, sent from `OWNER_EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`; a tenant with no owner is stamped and logged (`downgrade_grace.owner_missing`), and a failed send is logged (`downgrade_grace.email_send_failed`) and not retried. On day 14 the sweep first ends the grace with `endDowngradeGrace`, so a site that paid while the sweep ran is left alone, then locks the memberships over the plan's seats (`lockMembershipsOverSeats`), stops the pending invites over them (`stopPendingInvitesOverSeats`) and, on a plan without `hasCustomDomain`, deletes any pending custom-domain claim and points `primaryDomain` back at the tenant's subdomain with `setTenantPrimaryDomain`, purging the site's cache; the custom domain's `tenant_domains` row stays connected. A site with no subdomain is logged (`downgrade_grace.subdomain_missing`). A tenant that upgrades during the grace has its grace cleared by the plan change, so it gets no further email and nothing locks, and one that upgrades after day 14 gets back the members that fit its new seats; a failing tenant is logged (`downgrade_grace.step_failed`) with its step. When locking or the address switch fails after the grace end is claimed, the sweep gives the claim back with `restoreDowngradeGrace`, which writes the original start (and `downgraded_from_plan`) back only while the site is still `FREE` with no grace, and logs `downgrade_grace.grace_end_failed` when it was given back or giving it back failed; the next sweep finds the grace due again and retries day 14. A site that paid in between is left untouched and logs nothing, since the write that fails for it is the plan check, not a fault (decided, owner, 2026-11-13).
- A reader account exists only while `READER_ACCOUNTS` is on, so a `FREE` or `STARTER` site has none.
- Saving a tenant's details (plan, default language, domain) or its additional languages calls the revalidation route, so the site serves the change on its next load. A failed call is logged and does not fail the save; the site then catches up within 3600s.
- `TENANT_PLAN` lives in `@blog/config` because `@blog/studio` reads it; `PLAN_REGISTRY` and the other plan maps stay in `@blog/db`, read only by `db` and the apps above it.
- `TENANT_PLAN` backs a pgEnum: reordering it reorders the enum, and a new plan needs a migration plus entries in every `Record<TTenantPlan, …>` map.
- A new Growth-only Look column joins `LOOK_FIELDS` and the effective-Look fallback in the change that adds it.
- A new capability means a `CAPABILITY` key, every `Record<TCapability, …>` map, a `settings_features` column with its migration, a `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES` row and the mapping in `to-effective-settings-features`.
