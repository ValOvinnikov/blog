# Plans and entitlements

## What it does

Every tenant is on one plan, `FREE`, `STARTER` or `GROWTH`, which an operator sets. The plan is a ceiling: it decides which reader-facing capabilities the site may switch on, how many languages it serves, and which tenant pages the owner sees in the platform. Inside that ceiling the owner switches each capability on or off on the Features page, and the site shows a capability only when both the plan and the toggle allow it. A site created through self-serve onboarding starts on `GROWTH` in status `TRIAL`, with `trial_ends_at` 14 days (`TRIAL_LENGTH_DAYS`) after creation and no Stripe subscription. While the site has a live subscription (a stored subscription id on a plan in `SELLABLE_TENANT_PLANS`), the plan follows Stripe and changes only on the owner's Billing page or in the Stripe dashboard ([billing](./billing.md)); otherwise it is a column an operator edits. A trial that ends without a subscription drops to `FREE`, and any site that leaves a paid plan or a trial for `FREE` gets a 14-day downgrade grace before its extra team members lock and its custom domain stops being the main address. A super admin can also grant one site a single capability, module pack, Look field or extra languages outside its plan; a grant raises the ceiling, never switches anything on, and is never charged.

## Surfaces and routes

- **Platform, operator** — the plan picker in the Add tenant form and the tenant details panel; the plan shows in the tenants table and on the owner's home card. A saved details change refreshes the tenants table (`revalidatePath` on the `(operator)/tenants` page), so it shows the new plan without a reload, and a changed plan is recorded in the audit event as `planChanged: { from, to }`, which Activity shows as "Plan: {from} → {to}" in place of "Plan" among the changed fields.
- **Owner email** — a saved plan change emails the owner once (see Configuration). The downgrade grace emails the owner on day 0, 7 and 13 (see Constraints and invariants).
- **Platform, Features page** — `/dashboard/features` (owner) and `/tenants/{id}/features` (operator). Six capability rows: Reader accounts is the main switch, with Bookmarks indented under it and disabled while it is off. Comments and Ratings have no row on any plan, for the owner or the operator (`SHOWN_CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`): ratings are dropped and comments are hidden until built. A row outside the plan is locked with a badge naming the cheapest plan that holds it (`lowestPlanWithCapability`): "Starter plan" for the contact form on Free, "Growth plan" for the rest. While Reader accounts is off, a note says nobody new can sign in.
- **Platform, plan-gated pages** — `/dashboard/languages` and `/dashboard/email` answer 404 when the plan does not open them. Subscribers and Team are pages, opened as Plan page access below says; the sidebar has no Comments entry.
- **Platform, Sign-in page** — `/dashboard/sign-in` (owner) and `/tenants/{id}/sign-in` (operator, read-only), right after Domain in the Configuration menu on every plan. One card per provider (Google, then GitHub) with its status pill ("Shared app" or "Your own app") and the exact callback address, `https://<main address>/api/auth/callback/<provider>`, with Copy. A Growth owner adds, replaces and removes a client ID and secret per provider; a Free or Starter site's owner sees the Growth note above two locked rows, whose "See what's included" lists your own Google app and your own GitHub app as plain items and whose "Ask about Growth" opens the Upgrade page ([billing](./billing.md)); the operator view shows no note.
- **Platform, entitlement grants (super admin only)** — on the operator's `/tenants/{id}` Features, Look and Languages pages, a live tenant's plan-locked, non-coming-soon capability row and Look field get a small "Grant to this tenant" button, and the Languages "Plan limit" notice gets one "Grant more languages" once the plan's allowance is used and fewer than four additional languages are allowed. On a plan that publishes the default only, its dialog offers 1–4 additional languages; on one that already allows some (Growth), only the counts above it, with its own description (owner addition to board 5, 2026-11-18). The super admin sees the notice at the allowance even where the owner sees none. Each opens a `ConfirmDialog` (tone PRIMARY) with an Expires choice — Never (default), 30 days, 90 days or 1 year — and an optional note (500 characters). The tenant Overview has a **Grants** card after Tenant details, visible to every operator: each unrevoked grant with its kind, granter, date and expiry or "Until revoked", its note, an "Expired" badge with "Remove" once its end has passed, and "Included in the plan" once the plan covers it; a super admin also gets "Revoke" per row and "Grant a module pack" (packs the plan includes, or already granted, are listed but disabled). Module packs are never granted from inside Studio. After a grant, revoke or remove the page refreshes and focus moves to a stable element: the row's switch, the Look field's name (without its Optional tag or unsaved marker), or the Grants or Languages card heading (`ConfirmDialog`'s `returnFocusId`), or the page's main region when that element is gone; Cancel returns focus to the button that opened the dialog. The controls never show on the owner's `/dashboard` tree, to an admin or moderator, or for an archived tenant.
- **Platform, owner's view of a grant** — an item unlocked only by a grant shows a "Granted" badge ("Granted until {date}" when it expires) in place of its plan lock on Features and Look; a language grant removes the Plan limit notice, the allowance reads "{used} of {n} additional languages in use." and "Granted" sits in the Languages card header. When the plan already includes the item, nothing shows.
- **Platform, owner email on a grant or revoke** — after a super admin's grant or revoke is saved, the owner (the invited address until they sign in) gets one email from the owner sender, in the site's default language, with an "Open your dashboard" button. A grant names the item in the Grants card's words, its end date or that it lasts until revoked, and that nothing is charged; a revoke names the item and says nothing is deleted. A language-grant raise sends one grant email for the new count and none for the grant it replaced; removing a grant that had already ended by its date sends nothing. A failed send is logged (`entitlement_grants.email_failed`) and never fails the grant or revoke.
- **Platform, Usage card** — on the owner's `/dashboard` Overview and the operator's `/tenants/{id}` Overview (not for an archived tenant), one row per metered allowance the plan or a grant includes, in this order: AI takeaways this month (always; `countTakeawayGenerationsThisMonth` of `takeawaysPerMonth`), Emails sent this month (when the entitlements hold `NEWSLETTER`; `getNewsletterLimits`' `sentThisMonth` of the allowance the worker's send gate enforces), Team seats (when `seats` > 1; `countSeatsInUse` of `seats`) and Additional languages (when `localeLimit` > 1; the live additional locales of `localeLimit` − 1). Every allowance comes from the resolved entitlements, never the plan record alone, and a row a grant raised carries the "Granted" badge. Each row shows "{used} of {allowance}", capped at the allowance, a `role="meter"` bar and a note: under 80% a plain note (monthly rows: the reset date, the first of the next UTC month); from 80% (rounded down) the percentage and what is left in `warn`; at 100% the used-up note, `bad` on the monthly rows and `warn` on seats and languages. The languages row links to Languages and the seats row to Team (on `/dashboard`, only for a viewer who can manage, since Team 404s for the rest); the emails row gains its Subscribers link and paused-for-the-month state with the Subscribers page. A failed read shows that row's warning and "Couldn't load this count…" and is logged once (`usage.meter_read_failed`); the rest of the card renders. The card streams behind a `DetailCardSkeleton`. On a Free or Starter site the real owner's card has a footer note (the shared `CardFooterNote`) linking the plan comparison (`pricingPageUrl`); members and the operator view get none.
- **Public site** — no route of its own. It gates `/bookmarks` (404 unless Reader accounts and Bookmarks are both on) and `/account` (404 there only to a signed-out visitor on a site with Reader accounts off), the header account menu, the bookmark button, the newsletter module and its server action, and the consent banner. The owner's own analytics tool follows `hasOwnAnalytics` (see [consent-and-analytics](./consent-and-analytics.md)). A page renders only the module types its tenant's plan entitles.
- **Public site, Compare plans** — the marketing site's pricing page (`/pricing#compare`) shows every plan's entitlements side by side, generated from these records; it is the one public full list, and owner plan emails link to it ([pricing-module](./pricing-module.md)).
- **Studio** — a module type whose capability is off is relabelled "(off in Features)" in pickers, and its documents carry a warning; `module_newsletter` and `module_contactForm` map to a capability. Given module-pack entitlements, a module type outside them shows as unavailable in a page's module picker: every plan's "Add item…" menu is the same picker — search, the All and kind tabs, the same rows, icons and order, each row titled in bold with its module's own title — with the module disabled in its usual place and a badge naming its pack's plan ("Starter" for the contact form, "Growth" for the rest) at the right of its row, while the per-row "Add item before/after" menus (Sanity's own, which can't be replaced) list it last with a lock and a "— Starter plan" or "— Growth plan" title. It offers no existing document to pick there, and can't be created new from the desk, the global Create menu or a reference's "Create new"; its existing documents stay listed and editable, with a banner, and a page referencing one shows a warning on that item that never blocks publishing. When a type is both off-plan and off in Features, the plan message shows.

## Data

- `tenants.plan` — required, the `tenant_plan` pgEnum built from `TENANT_PLAN`.
- `PLAN_REGISTRY` (`@shinefold/db/constants`) — `FREE`: `CONSENT_BANNER`. `STARTER`: `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM`. `GROWTH`: all eight `CAPABILITY` keys (`READER_ACCOUNTS`, `COMMENTS`, `RATINGS`, `BOOKMARKS`, `NEWSLETTER`, `ANALYTICS`, `CONSENT_BANNER`, `CONTACT_FORM`). Ratings is Growth only because rating needs sign-in.
- `PLAN_LOCALE_LIMIT` — languages per plan including the default: `FREE` 1, `STARTER` 1, `GROWTH` 3.
- `PLAN_MONTHLY_SENDS_PER_SUBSCRIBER` and `SUBSCRIBER_BAND_CEILINGS` (1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000) — the monthly newsletter allowance: `FREE` 0, `STARTER` 0, `GROWTH` 6 × the band ceiling. `monthlySendAllowance(plan, bandCeiling)` applies it. A `NEWSLETTER` grant on `FREE` or `STARTER` sends at `GROWTH`'s rate ([newsletter](./newsletter.md)). `getNewsletterLimits(tenant)` (`queries/email-usage/`) reads a site's newsletter limits for the worker's send gate and any page that shows them: the active-subscriber count, the band ceiling (the stored `subscriber_band_ceiling`, or 1,000 when none is set), this UTC month's newsletter post sends, and the allowance from that ceiling at the rate the resolved entitlements give.
- `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS` (`@shinefold/db/constants`) — one record per plan. `lookFields` lists the `site_config` Look columns (`LOOK_FIELDS`) the plan may set: `FREE` and `STARTER` the preset, accent hue, brand name, logo, dark-mode logo, favicon and Cookie settings button; `GROWTH` every Look column. `modulePacks` lists the module packs the plan entitles: `FREE` `CORE`; `STARTER` `CORE` and `STARTER`; `GROWTH` all three. `hasPoweredByMark` says whether the site's footer carries the "Powered by" mark: `FREE` yes, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` no (see [footer-and-legal-pages](./footer-and-legal-pages.md)). `hasCustomDomain` says whether the owner may connect their own domain and make it the main address: `FREE` no, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` yes; the Domain page's card and the connect, verify and make-main actions read it, and none compares against a plan name. `hasOwnOAuthApps` says whether the site signs readers in through its own Google and GitHub apps when it has saved credentials for them: `FREE` and `STARTER` no, `GROWTH` yes (see [reader-auth](./reader-auth.md)). `hasOwnAnalytics` says whether the site loads the owner's own analytics tool: `FREE` no, `STARTER` and `GROWTH` yes; `getTenantAnalytics` reads it (see [consent-and-analytics](./consent-and-analytics.md)). `seats` is how many people the site may have in its team, counting the owner, every membership and every pending invite: `FREE` 1, `STARTER` 1, `GROWTH` 3. `countSeatsInUse(tenantId)` returns that count — every membership, locked ones included, plus every unconsumed invite — from the same SQL the invite seat limit checks. `countUnlockedSeatsInUse(tenantId)` returns the same count without locked memberships: the unlocked memberships plus every unconsumed invite. It decides whether a site is over its seats for Team's page access ([tenant-access](./tenant-access.md)). The epic's later entitlements join this record as fields of their own.
- **Presets** — `OPEN_PRESET_IDS` (`@shinefold/config`), Console and Editorial, are the presets every tenant may use; every other `PRESET_ID`, today `SHINEFOLD` (`GRANTABLE_PRESET_IDS`), is used only by a tenant holding a grant for it. `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS`' `unlockedPresets` is empty on every plan, and the resolved entitlements' `unlockedPresets` add each open `PRESET` grant (`ENTITLEMENT_KIND.PRESET`, its key the preset id), so an expired or revoked grant unlocks nothing. `presetsWithin(entitlements)` (`@shinefold/db/utils/effective-look/effective-look`) lists the presets a tenant may use and `isPresetWithin` checks one against it. `toEffectiveLookWithin` gives a stored preset outside them Console, with Console's values for the locked fields, so the site, the Look form and its preview render Console as soon as a grant lapses. The stored choice is kept: saving the Console shown in its place leaves the stored preset as it is, and it returns with a new grant. The Preset card lists `presetsWithin`, `updateLookAction` refuses a switch to a preset the tenant may not use (a `NOT_IN_PLAN` refusal naming `preset`), and signup refuses any preset outside `OPEN_PRESET_IDS`.
- `tenant_oauth_credentials` — one row per tenant and provider: `client_id`, the secret encrypted under `TENANT_OAUTH_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, and nullable `callback_domain`, the main address the owner last registered (null reads as the current one). Saving a card, or "I've added it", sets it to the current main address.
- `tenant_analytics` — at most one row per tenant (`tenant_id` primary key, cascade delete): `provider` (`ANALYTICS_PROVIDER`) and `analytics_id`, both `NOT NULL`; no row means no analytics.
- `settings_features` — one row per tenant (unique `tenant_id`, cascade delete), eight `NOT NULL` boolean toggles. Provisioning writes no row; an absent row resolves to the tenant's current preset's `featureDefaults` (both presets: bookmarks and contact form on; reader accounts, comments, ratings, newsletter, analytics and consent banner off). The column defaults match.
- `MODULE_PACK` (`CORE`, `STARTER`, `GROWTH`) and `MODULE_PACK_BY_TYPE` (`@shinefold/config/constants`) give every module `_type` exactly one pack; the map `satisfies Record<TModuleType, TModulePack>`, so a new module type fails type-check until it has one. Core: the three heroes, the five Posts modules, content, FAQ, embed, section pages, CTA, newsletter and cookie settings. Starter: contact form. Growth: testimonial, team, logo wall, stats, pricing, feature list, feature highlights and timeline. `isModuleTypeEntitled(type, packs)` is the one check; an unknown type is never entitled. `CAPABILITY_BY_MODULE_TYPE` (`module_newsletter` → `NEWSLETTER`, `module_contactForm` → `CONTACT_FORM`) names the capability a module type also needs; `getModuleCapability(type)` reads it, for the Studio's requirement warnings and the pricing page's plan comparison.
- `pricingPageUrl(marketingSiteUrl)` (`@shinefold/config`) — the address of the marketing pricing page's plan comparison, `/pricing#compare` (`routes.pricingComparison()`) on the marketing site's URL. It is the one public place that lists each plan's features, so an owner email about a plan links here instead of listing them; both apps call it with the marketing site's address and neither retypes the path (decided, owner, 2026-11-18).
- `SELLABLE_TENANT_PLANS` (`@shinefold/config/constants`) — the plans sold through Stripe, today `STARTER` and `GROWTH`; `@shinefold/studio` reads it, so the Studio names no plan. `PLAN_PRICE_LOOKUP_KEYS` is typed so a sellable plan must have lookup keys and every other plan must be `null`; the two cannot drift.
- `PLAN_PRICE_LOOKUP_KEYS` (`@shinefold/db/constants`) — each plan's Stripe Price lookup keys, never Price ids or amounts: `FREE` none; `STARTER` `STARTER_MONTHLY` and `STARTER_YEARLY`, with no band keys; `GROWTH` `GROWTH_MONTHLY`, `GROWTH_YEARLY` and, per paid subscriber band above the included 1,000, a monthly and a yearly key: `GROWTH_BAND_2500` and `GROWTH_BAND_2500_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_5000` and `GROWTH_BAND_5000_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_10000` and `GROWTH_BAND_10000_YEARLY`, `GROWTH_BAND_25000` and `GROWTH_BAND_25000_YEARLY`. These twelve strings are the lookup keys set on the Prices in Stripe. The band keys sit in a full record over the paid band ceilings of `SUBSCRIBER_BAND_CEILINGS`, so a new ceiling fails type-check until it has both keys. Every item on one Stripe subscription bills on one interval, so a yearly Growth subscription carries the yearly band Price; a yearly band costs ten times the monthly one. A key names the same Price in Stripe's test and live modes, and each Price carries dollars, euros and pounds as currency options; the amounts live only in Stripe, and a price change moves the key to a new Price with no deploy. The record is `Record<TTenantPlan, …>`, so a new plan fails type-check until it has its keys. The Stripe webhook reads it to map a subscription back to a plan and band ([billing](./billing.md)).
- Billing state on `tenants`, all nullable: `stripe_customer_id` and `stripe_subscription_id` (each unique), `stripe_promotion_code` (the code the subscription started with, for campaign attribution), `subscriber_band_ceiling` (the paid subscriber band the Stripe subscription carries, 2,500 to 25,000, read from its band Price; null means the included 1,000), `trial_ends_at`, `downgrade_grace_started_at`, and one sent-at stamp per grace email (`grace_started_email_sent_at`, `grace_halfway_email_sent_at`, `grace_ending_email_sent_at`). `getTenantBilling` and `setTenantBilling` read and write them; in a write, a field left out keeps its value and `null` clears it. `setTenantPlan(tenantId, { plan, status })` is the plan-change write the webhook uses. It writes only an `ACTIVE`, `TRIAL` or `PAST_DUE` tenant (`WRITABLE_TENANT_STATUSES`) and returns a `TENANT_PLAN_WRITE_OUTCOME`: `WRITTEN` with the stored plan and status, `NOT_WRITABLE` with the current status for an archived or suspended tenant, which it leaves unchanged, or `NOT_FOUND`; it and `updateTenantDetails` clear `self_serve_owner_email_index` in the same statement on any move to a paid plan ([platform sign-in and onboarding](./platform-sign-in-and-onboarding.md)). `getTenantIdByStripeIds({ stripeCustomerId?, stripeSubscriptionId? })` returns a `STRIPE_TENANT_LOOKUP_OUTCOME`: `FOUND` with the `tenantId` of the tenant carrying either id, so a tenant whose subscription id is not stored yet is still found; `CONFLICT` with `customerTenantId` and `subscriptionTenantId` when the two ids name different tenants, which the caller logs and writes nothing for; `NOT_FOUND` when no tenant carries either id, or when neither id is given (an empty string counts as not given) without querying.
- Downgrade grace: `planChangeFields(plan, status, now)` sets `downgrade_grace_started_at` to now, records the plan the site left in `downgraded_from_plan`, and clears the three email stamps, when a site leaves a paid plan or a trial for `FREE` (its current plan is not `FREE`, or its status is `TRIAL`), or moves to a paid plan with fewer `seats` than its current plan while its seats in use (`countSeatsInUse`'s count) exceed the new plan's `seats` (Growth → Starter with a member or pending invite beside the owner; decided, owner, 2026-11-24). A grace already running survives any move to a plan whose `seats` still don't fit the site's seats in use, keeping its original deadline, stamps and `downgraded_from_plan`, and re-targets to the new plan: day 14 enforces the plan the site is on then (decided, owner, 2026-11-24). A move to `FREE` always keeps a running grace. A move to a paid plan whose seats fit — back up, or after members were removed — clears the grace, its stamps and `downgraded_from_plan`. `updateTenantDetails` and `setTenantPlan` both write through it, so an operator plan change, the Stripe webhook and the trial end all start or end the grace the same way, and the same statement unlocks the locked memberships that fit the new plan's seats (`unlockMembershipsWithinPlan`; `setTenantPlan` only when it writes the plan). An operator moving a `TRIAL` site to `FREE` through `updateTenantDetails` ends the trial as `endTenantTrial` does, writing `ACTIVE` in the same statement, so the owner sees no trial notice; every other operator plan change keeps the site's status, and a save that fails after the row is written restores it. `endTenantTrial(tenantId, now)` moves a `TRIAL` tenant past `trial_ends_at` with no `stripe_subscription_id` and not archived to `FREE` + `ACTIVE`, keeping its `self_serve_owner_email_index` (a trial and a Free site share the slot), and returns the row only to the caller that ended it; `listTenantsWithEndedTrial(now)` lists the same tenants. Grace days are counted in whole UTC days from the day the grace started, so the time a sweep runs never moves a step to another day. `GRACE_EMAIL` (`STARTED`, `HALFWAY`, `ENDING`) is due from `GRACE_EMAIL_DAY` (0, 7, 13) until the next email's day, the last until `DOWNGRADE_GRACE_DAYS` (14); `listTenantsDueForGraceEmail(email, now)` lists the tenants whose email is due and unstamped, and `claimGraceEmail(tenantId, email, now)` stamps it in one conditional update, returning the row only to the caller that claimed it. `listTenantsDueForGraceEnd(now)` lists the tenants on day 14 or later, and `endDowngradeGrace(tenantId, graceStartedAt)` clears the grace, its stamps and `downgraded_from_plan` only while the stored start still matches — a plan change since it was read has cleared or restarted it otherwise — on whichever plan the grace runs, returning the row only to the caller that ended it.
- `memberships.locked_at` — nullable. Every day-14 write — the lock, stopping invites, `setTenantPrimaryDomain` and `deleteCustomDomainClaim` given a plan — applies only while the tenant is still on that plan, checked in the same statement (`isTenantOnPlan`), so a payment that lands mid-sweep is never undone; a failed operator details save re-locks only the members its unlock released, never one the grace left unlocked. A locked membership is kept, never deleted, and is invisible to `getMembership` and `listMembershipsWithTenantsForUser`, so its member cannot reach the site's admin. Seats rank the owner first and the rest by when they joined. `lockMembershipsOverSeats(tenantId, plan, now)` locks the unlocked memberships past the plan's seats and returns how many it locked; `countMembershipsOverSeats(tenantId, seats)` counts them without locking. `stopPendingInvitesOverSeats(tenantId, plan)` deletes the unaccepted non-owner invites, latest first, that no longer fit beside the unlocked memberships. `consumeMembershipInvite` claims a non-owner invite only while the tenant's unlocked memberships are under its plan's seats, leaving it pending otherwise, and after the insert locks the member who joined last if two accepts raced past the seats.
- `stripe_webhook_events` — one row per handled Stripe event id (`event_id` primary key, `handled_at`). `recordStripeEvent(eventId)` returns `RECORDED` the first time and `ALREADY_HANDLED` for a redelivered or replayed id, so of two concurrent deliveries exactly one is recorded. Recording marks the event handled, so the webhook records it only after its own writes succeed; a failed handler then leaves the id unrecorded and Stripe's retry is processed.
- `stripe_prices` — each plan lookup key's amount per `BILLING_CURRENCY` (`USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`) in minor units, with its Stripe Price and product ids; primary key `(lookup_key, currency)`. `saveStripePrice` upserts a key's amounts and drops the currencies the Price no longer carries in one statement; `removeStripePrice` drops a Price's rows; `removeStripePricesExcept` drops every row whose Price is not in the given list; `listStripePrices` reads them all. `stripe_promotion_codes` — each active promotion code (stored upper case; saving a code deletes any older promotion code that reused its string in the same statement, and a read takes the newest) with its `percent_off` or per-currency `amount_off_by_currency`, its coupon's `duration` (`STRIPE_COUPON_DURATION`: `ONCE`, `REPEATING`, `FOREVER`) and `duration_in_months`, the product ids it applies to (none = every product) and its expiry; `getActiveStripePromotionCode` matches a code case-blind and ignores an expired one; `removeStripePromotionCodesExcept` drops every code not in the given list. Only a code anyone may redeem belongs here: the webhook skips, or removes, a code restricted to one customer, to first-time buyers or to a minimum amount, or whose `max_redemptions` is reached, or whose coupon is amount-off in none of the `BILLING_CURRENCY` currencies. The webhook saves the Price or promotion code it re-reads from Stripe, never the event payload: a late event for an old Price still carries the lookup key it has since lost, and saving it would put the old amounts back. The Stripe webhook keeps both current, the operator's sync script fills them and prunes what Stripe no longer lists ([billing](./billing.md)), and `apps/web` reads them, never Stripe ([pricing-module](./pricing-module.md)).
- **Caching (web)** — `getTenantEntitlements` caches the tenant's plan and its open entitlement grants together, and `getEffectiveSettingsFeatures` the owner's toggles, both `unstable_cache` per tenant, tagged `tenant-plan:<id>` and `settings-features:<id>`, with `TENANT_CONFIG_REVALIDATE_SECONDS` (86400) as the fallback. Grants are resolved against the clock on every read (`resolveTenantEntitlements`), never at cache fill, so a grant stops applying the moment it expires with no purge. The site reads grants with `listResolvableEntitlementGrants`, which selects only what the resolver needs, with no granter join and no decryption. When the grants cannot be read, live-language routing resolves the plan alone and logs `tenant_entitlements.grants_fetch_failed`. `POST /api/revalidate-site-config` expires both tags with `site-config:<id>` and purges that tenant's own pages ([rendering-and-caching](./rendering-and-caching.md)); the grant screen (#6783) calls it on every grant and revoke, so pages, gates and the sitemap serve the change on their next request. The proxy's locale routing reads the grants through its own cache entry (`resolveProxyTenantPlanEntitlements`, grants only), tagged `site-config:<id>` with a one-hour revalidate, so the same call clears it and the hour is only the fallback. The platform reads the plan and the open grants uncached once per tenant request: `requireTenantMembership`, `requireTenantById`, `getPlatformTenant` and the dashboard's active tenant resolve them with `resolveTenantEntitlements` into `TPlatformTenant.entitlements` and `pageAccess`, which every platform lock, save, nav entry and Studio read. Tenant lists (the switcher, the tenant picker, the operator's Tenants list) use `toPlatformTenantSummary`, which reads no grants.

## Configuration

- **Operator** (any `admins` row) sets the plan through `createTenantAction` and `updateTenantDetailsAction`, both behind `requireAdmin`. The plan stays editable in the tenant details panel once provisioning has succeeded, and locks while provisioning is running, on an archived tenant, and while the site has a live subscription (`hasStoredSubscription`), when a note says the plan follows Stripe and changes on the owner's Billing page or in the Stripe dashboard. `updateTenantDetailsAction` refuses a plan change for such a site (`PLAN_FOLLOWS_SUBSCRIPTION`) and still saves its other fields. A change applies on the site's next page load and deletes no stored data; a downgrade only hides what the new plan does not hold.
- **Plan change email** — when `updateTenantDetailsAction` saves a plan that differs from the stored one, or the Stripe webhook moves a site to another plan ([billing](./billing.md)), its caller runs `onTenantPlanChange(tenantId, fromPlan, toPlan)` — the one step every plan change runs — in `after()`, once the response has gone; it calls `notifyOwnerOfPlanChange`, which emails the owner through `@shinefold/email`'s `buildOwnerPlanChangeEmail`: the notice for the new plan, worded to read right whichever plan it came from: it names the new plan and says nothing is deleted, with an "Open your dashboard" button to the tenant's dashboard and a "See what each plan includes" link to the pricing page's plan comparison. It lists no features. The link is `pricingPageUrl` on `MARKETING_SITE_URL`, or on the root domain's apex without it (`toMarketingSiteUrl`, which the platform's legal links use too), so every platform link to the marketing site points at the same place (decided, owner, 2026-11-17); with neither, the email goes without the link. The copy is fixed platform copy (never Voice), in the `ownerPlanChangeEmail` namespace of the platform's message catalogues, written in the site's default language (`tenants.locale`). It goes to `getTenantOwnerEmail`, which is the pending owner-invite address until the owner joins, from `OWNER_EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`. With no sender address or `PLATFORM_APP_BASE_URL` it is skipped and logged (`tenants.plan_change_email_unconfigured`); with no owner address too (`tenants.plan_change_email_owner_missing`). A failed send is logged (`tenants.plan_change_email_failed`) and never fails or undoes the save.
- **Growth layer on upgrade** — `onTenantPlanChange` also runs `enqueueGrowthLayerOnUpgrade`, alongside the email, so an operator's change and a Stripe upgrade trigger it alike: on a move from a plan whose `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS` lack the `GROWTH` module pack to one that holds it, it sends the worker a signed `POST /api/growth-layer/apply`, which adds the site type's Growth sections ([site templates](./site-templates.md#growth-layer)). A move between plans that both hold the pack, or to one without it, sends nothing, and a downgrade removes nothing. A 409 needs nothing — a site not yet provisioned gets the layer when seeding reads its new plan — and any other response but 202 is logged (`growth_layer.apply_enqueue_rejected`); an unreachable worker is logged by `enqueueWorkerRequest`. Neither fails or undoes the plan change. A module-pack grant is not a plan change and does not apply the layer.
- **Grants** — `grantEntitlementAction` and `revokeEntitlementGrantAction` (`apps/platform/src/server/entitlement-grants/`) run behind `requireSuperAdmin`, so any other role or an owner is refused on the server (404). A grant refuses a coming-soon capability (`COMING_SOON`), an item the plan already includes (`INCLUDED_IN_PLAN`), an archived or unknown tenant, and a second open grant of the same item (`ALREADY_GRANTED`). A higher language count replaces the open language grant in one statement (`raiseLocaleLimitGrant`: the lower grant closes and the higher opens together, so the site never drops to its plan's limit in between) and `grantEntitlement` returns the closed grant's id as `closedGrantId`; a lower or equal one is refused. A raise never shortens a grant: one whose expiry is earlier than the open language grant's is refused with `DB_ENTITLEMENT_EXPIRY_SHORTENED` (`EXPIRY_SHORTENED` in the action, whose message tells the super admin to pick a later expiry or revoke that grant first) and the open grant stays open, where Never counts as the latest expiry (decided, owner, 2026-11-18). Every grant kind binds `expires_at` through the column's encoder, so it is stored in UTC whatever the server's time zone. `toStoredEntitlementItem` (`@shinefold/db`) is the one mapping from a grant item to its stored kind, key and value; `expires_at` is the grant time plus the chosen period (`grantExpiresAt`), or null for Never. Revoke (and Remove on an expired grant) closes the row with `revoked_at`/`revoked_by` and deletes nothing, so granting again restores what the tenant set up. Both write `ENTITLEMENT_GRANTED` or `ENTITLEMENT_REVOKED` to the audit log with the grant's kind, key, value, expiry and note, only after the write succeeds; a raise also writes `ENTITLEMENT_REVOKED` for the grant it closed, with its id, kind, key and `replacedBy` (the new grant's id), and call `revalidateSiteConfig(tenantId)`, so the site and its proxy read the change on the next request (decided, owner, 2026-11-18). The tenant's activity list names the item of each grant and revoke in the Grants card's words (`useGrantItemLabel`), with its expiry or "Until revoked" and its note when the details hold them; a raise's closing revoke names only the item ("Additional languages"), since its details hold no count. Granting a Reader accounts sub-feature also grants `READER_ACCOUNTS`. A failed write is logged (`entitlement_grants.grant_failed`, `revoke_failed`). `listEntitlementGrants` returns every unrevoked grant, expired ones included, for the Grants card.
- **Owner or operator** saves Features through `updateFeaturesAction`, behind `requireTenantMembership` (any member; an operator gets a virtual owner membership).
- **Coming soon** — `COMMENTS` and `RATINGS` (`COMING_SOON_CAPABILITIES`) carry `isComingSoon` in `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`, and the save writes them off whatever the payload says. Neither is shown: both are left out of `SHOWN_CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`, and their stored toggles and columns stay. An owner can switch `READER_ACCOUNTS`, `BOOKMARKS`, `NEWSLETTER`, `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM` on `GROWTH`, `CONSENT_BANNER` and `CONTACT_FORM` on `STARTER`, and only `CONSENT_BANNER` on `FREE`. `ANALYTICS` has no switch: its row takes the owner's own analytics tool, on `STARTER` and `GROWTH` (`hasOwnAnalytics`; see [consent-and-analytics](./consent-and-analytics.md)).
- **Reader accounts** — `BOOKMARKS`, `COMMENTS` and `RATINGS` carry `parentField: 'readerAccountsEnabled'` in `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES`. While the parent is off, a sub-item can be saved off but never on (`blockSwitchOnWhileParentOff`), so its stored value comes back when the parent is switched on again, and `getEnabledCapabilities` reports it off (`withParentOffApplied`). Look's account options and the public site's sign-in both follow `READER_ACCOUNTS`, and on the public site a sub-item applies only while it is on (`isReaderAccountFeatureEnabled`).
- **Plan page access** (`entitlementPageAccess`, from the resolved entitlements) — Languages when `localeLimit` > 1; Email when the entitlements hold bookmarks, comments, newsletter or contact form; Leads with contact form; Subscribers with newsletter; Team when `seats` is more than 1 (`GROWTH` only today), or on any plan while its unlocked members plus pending invites exceed its `seats` (`resolveTenantPageAccess` adds this, for a viewer who can manage; Team then offers Remove and Cancel invite but no Invite, see [tenant-access](./tenant-access.md)). No page gate names a plan. The owner's sidebar omits the rest and their pages 404; the operator's `/tenants/{id}` view shows every entry and page whatever the plan.
- **Look** — fields outside `lookFields` show locked on the Look page with a Growth badge beside the label, at the preset's value, focusable and announced as unavailable (see [look](./look.md)). On a plan with locked fields (`FREE` and `STARTER`) the owner also sees the Growth note, and the reset-to-preset action is hidden; the note's "See what's included" lists what Growth adds on the page.
- **Module packs in Studio** — `StudioMount` takes an optional plain-data `entitlements` object whose `modulePacks` lists the entitled packs. Omitted, every pack is entitled: the site is the enforcement point, and the Sanity CLI Studio has no tenant. The platform's `StudioMountView` passes the resolved `modulePacks` (the plan's plus any granted pack), read on every load, so the owner's `/dashboard` Studio and the operator's `/tenants/{id}` Studio both follow the current plan and grants.
- **Module packs on the site** — `renderModules` wraps each module in `ModuleGate`, and `renderHeroModule` runs the same check: `isModuleEntitled` reads `getRequestContext().tenantId` and the cached `getTenantEntitlements`, once per request, and renders a module only when `isModuleTypeEntitled` passes for the resolved `modulePacks` (the plan's plus any granted pack). A skipped module renders nothing, and its component never runs, so its data is never fetched. With no tenant (the local and dev platform fallback) every module renders. A downgrade deletes no content; an upgrade shows the modules again once the plan cache refreshes.
- **Powered-by mark** — `SiteFooter` draws `Footer.PoweredBy` when `getPoweredByMark` finds the plan's `hasPoweredByMark` set. It reads the resolved entitlements through `getEffectiveEntitlements`, the cached `getTenantEntitlements` with Free's when they cannot be read (`site_config.plan_fetch_failed`), the same reader `getSiteConfig` uses for the Look. No call site compares against `FREE`.
- **Own sign-in apps** — `saveSignInAppAction`, `removeSignInAppAction` and `confirmSignInCallbackAction` (`apps/platform/src/server/sign-in-apps/`) run behind `requireSignInAppOwner` (the site's real owner only) and refuse an archived site; the save also refuses a plan without `hasOwnOAuthApps`. A save without a secret changes only the client ID of a saved app and keeps the stored secret. Save and remove revalidate the site and record `OAUTH_APP_SAVED`, `OAUTH_APP_REPLACED` or `OAUTH_APP_REMOVED` with the provider, never a credential. A saved secret never leaves the server: the page shows it as "Set". The full client ID reaches only the owner of an unarchived site, masked to its first six characters until Show; members and archived sites get the masked value, and the operator view none. After the main address changes, each own-app card warns until the owner saves it or confirms, and the Domain page's main-address confirmation names the providers with a saved app. A downgrade keeps the saved apps and shows "Your saved app is kept"; a kept app counts as the shared app everywhere, the operator view included, until an upgrade. Leaving the page with unsaved typing in either card asks first, counting the unsaved fields across both cards.
- **Not plan-gated** — Voice, Domain, Studio, the Features page itself, page types, AI takeaways.

## Constraints and invariants

- Most restrictive wins: `isCapabilityEnabled` is `entitlements.capabilities.includes(capability) && toggle`, over the resolved entitlements (plan and open grants). Every failure path returns `false`. A fetch error also logs (`settings_features.plan_fetch_failed` or `effective_fetch_failed`); a missing tenant or plan does not. A render site omits the capability silently, never with a placeholder.
- Modules on the site: when the plan can't be read, only `CORE` modules render and `module_renderer.plan_fetch_failed` is logged; a missing plan also falls back to `CORE` without a log. Nothing in the module path reads request headers, so ISR pages stay static.
- The server re-checks entitlement on every plan-gated save, and a disabled control is never the gate. Look (`updateLookAction`), Features (`updateFeaturesAction`) and Languages (`updateTenantLanguagesAction`) refuse a save only when it changes something the current plan does not include: a value different from both the stored one and the preset value the plan shows instead. An out-of-plan value still as stored never blocks a save, so a page opened before a downgrade can still save its allowed fields. A refused save writes nothing and returns one shape, `toNotInPlanRefusal` (`apps/platform/src/utils/plan-refusal/`): `REFUSED` with `SETTINGS_REFUSAL.NOT_IN_PLAN`, the tenant's current `plan` and the refused fields or languages. A later plan-gated action adopts it by returning that helper. Features logs the refusal as `settings_features.plan_entitlement_rejected`, Languages as its `UPDATE_LANGUAGES_REJECTED` event.
- The platform tells the owner at save time and nowhere else, with no polling or refresh on focus. `useSettingsForm` handles a `NOT_IN_PLAN` refusal for every settings page: it shows "Your site is on the {plan} plan, which doesn't include {items}, so nothing was saved." (`saveBar.notInPlan`), or a page's own message passed as `planRefusalMessageKey` — Languages says "Your site is on the {plan} plan, which allows {limit} languages, so {items} wasn't added." (`saveBar.languagesNotInPlan`, plural on the refused count, with the resolved limit — plan plus open grants — that the save action sends in the refusal's `localeLimit`) — labelling each item from the page's draft fields, and puts the refused items back to their saved values while keeping every other edit. It always calls `router.refresh()` once, so every lock on the page updates together; a revoked grant changes no plan, so the refusal's plan cannot tell whether the page is stale.
- A downgrade deletes nothing. Toggles are clamped on read: on the site, in Studio (`getEnabledCapabilities`) and in the Features page's initial values. Saving that page never writes an out-of-plan toggle: `updateFeaturesAction` keeps its stored value, so an upgrade brings back what was on. Switching on one that is stored off is refused.
- Look: `toEffectiveLookWithin` (`@shinefold/db/utils/effective-look/effective-look`, client-safe) gives each field outside the resolved `lookFields` its preset's value, in the platform's form and preview and in `apps/web`'s `getSiteConfig`. The Look form takes `lookFields` as part of its `tenant` prop. `updateLookAction` refuses a change to a field outside them (`listRefusedLookFields`) and otherwise writes only the fields they include (`pickLookWithin`), so a downgrade keeps the stored choices and an upgrade shows them again.
- Languages: the live set is the default plus the first `limit − 1` stored additional languages. `setTenantAdditionalLocales` reads the tenant's open grants itself, so the limit counts a language grant, and refuses to add a locale past it (`DB_LOCALE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) but lets a downgraded tenant keep or reorder its stored ones.
- Seats: `createMembershipInvite` refuses a new invite with the `MEMBERSHIP_INVITE_OUTCOME.SEAT_LIMIT` outcome, writing nothing, once the tenant's memberships plus pending invites reach the plan's `seats`. Two invites sent at once can both pass that check, so each re-counts the tenant's memberships plus pending invites in a fresh statement after inserting; while the count is over the plan's `seats`, the invite deletes itself and returns `SEAT_LIMIT`, whatever its rank. The tenant never ends over its limit: if two racing invites both see the overflow, both are refused and the owner retries. A caller sends the invite email only for a `CREATED` result. A re-invite of an address already invited reports that invite, even at the limit. Seating the owner at tenant creation, or through an owner invite, never checks the limit, because the owner is always within it. Accepting any other invite (`consumeMembershipInvite`) takes a seat only while the tenant's unlocked memberships are under its plan's seats, and leaves the invite pending otherwise. A downgrade deletes no membership: it refuses new invites until the tenant is under the limit, and when its grace ends it locks the memberships over the limit and stops the unaccepted invites that no longer fit.
- **Downgrade grace (worker).** The daily cron (`GET /api/inactivity-sweep`) runs `runDowngradeGraceSweep` after the inactivity stages, skipping the platform tenant, in this order: it ends every unpaid trial past its end (`endTenantTrial`) and purges that site's cache; it ends every grace 14 days old; then it sends the day-13, day-7 and day-0 emails, latest first, so a trial ended in this sweep gets its day-0 email in the same run and one sweep never sends two. Each email is claimed with `claimGraceEmail` before it is sent, so a second sweep the same day sends nothing twice. What will lock at day 14 is decided when each email is due, from the current plan's `PLAN_ENTITLEMENTS`: the memberships over its `seats` (`countMembershipsOverSeats`) and, on a plan without `hasCustomDomain`, a `primaryDomain` outside `TENANT_ROOT_DOMAIN`. The day-0 email is always sent: the site is on Free, and how to upgrade; when something will lock it also names it and the date. A grace on a paid plan (a move to fewer seats) has its own copy (`OWNER_GRACE_EMAIL_COPY[locale].fewerSeats`) that never says the site is on Free: each of its three emails goes only while members are over the plan's seats, lists by email address the unlocked members past the seats in seat order (`listTenantMembers`), and links the Team page (`OWNER_TEAM_PATH`) instead of the dashboard; it names no address change, since the plan keeps `hasCustomDomain`. No grace email lists what a plan includes: each links to the plan comparison instead, `pricingPageUrl` on `MARKETING_SITE_URL`, else on the `TENANT_ROOT_DOMAIN` apex (decided, owner, 2026-11-17). The day-7 and day-13 emails go only when something will lock, are neither claimed nor sent otherwise, and name what will lock (decided, owner, 2026-11-13). Their copy is fixed, in the site's default language (`OWNER_GRACE_EMAIL_COPY`), with the grace's end date written in that language and a button to the admin dashboard, sent from `OWNER_EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`; a tenant with no owner is stamped and logged (`downgrade_grace.owner_missing`), and a failed send is logged (`downgrade_grace.email_send_failed`) and not retried. On day 14 the sweep first ends the grace with `endDowngradeGrace`, so a site that paid while the sweep ran is left alone, then locks the memberships over the plan's seats (`lockMembershipsOverSeats`), stops the pending invites over them (`stopPendingInvitesOverSeats`) and, on a plan without `hasCustomDomain`, deletes any pending custom-domain claim and points `primaryDomain` back at the tenant's subdomain with `setTenantPrimaryDomain`, purging the site's cache; the custom domain's `tenant_domains` row stays connected and 301s to the subdomain ([multi-tenancy](./multi-tenancy.md)). A site with no subdomain is logged (`downgrade_grace.subdomain_missing`). A tenant that upgrades during the grace has its grace cleared by the plan change, so it gets no further email and nothing locks, and one that upgrades after day 14 gets back the members that fit its new seats; a failing tenant is logged (`downgrade_grace.step_failed`) with its step. When locking or the address switch fails after the grace end is claimed, the sweep gives the claim back with `restoreDowngradeGrace`, which writes the original start (and `downgraded_from_plan`) back only while the site is still on the plan the grace ended on, with no grace, and logs `downgrade_grace.grace_end_failed` when it was given back or giving it back failed; the next sweep finds the grace due again and retries day 14. A site that paid in between is left untouched and logs nothing, since the write that fails for it is the plan check, not a fault (decided, owner, 2026-11-13).
- A reader account exists only while `READER_ACCOUNTS` is on, so a `FREE` or `STARTER` site has none.
- Saving a tenant's details (plan, default language, domain) or its additional languages calls the revalidation route, so the site serves the change on its next load. A failed call is logged and does not fail the save; the site then catches up within 24 hours (`TENANT_CONFIG_REVALIDATE_SECONDS`).
- `TENANT_PLAN` lives in `@shinefold/config` because `@shinefold/studio` reads it; `PLAN_REGISTRY` and the other plan maps stay in `@shinefold/db`, read only by `db` and the apps above it.
- `TENANT_PLAN` backs a pgEnum: reordering it reorders the enum, and a new plan needs a migration plus entries in every `Record<TTenantPlan, …>` map.
- A new Growth-only Look column joins `LOOK_FIELDS` and the effective-Look fallback in the change that adds it.
- A new capability means a `CAPABILITY` key, every `Record<TCapability, …>` map, a `settings_features` column with its migration, a `CAPABILITY_TOGGLES` row and the mapping in `to-effective-settings-features`.
