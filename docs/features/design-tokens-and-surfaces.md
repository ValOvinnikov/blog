# Design tokens and surfaces

## What it does

The public site and `@shinefold/ui` draw every colour, size, radius, spacing step, shadow and duration from one token layer, `configs/tailwind/theme.css`, through Tailwind utilities. A tenant's Look overrides a fixed subset of those tokens at runtime; everything else is the same on every site. Coloured bands and cards set a few inherited properties, so focus-ring offsets, dividers and item-card frames match the surface they sit on without any component prop.

## Surfaces and routes

- No route. Every public page and every `@shinefold/ui` component consumes the tokens.
- `packages/ui`'s Storybook has a design-tokens story that parses `theme.css` (`token-registry.ts`, the `@role` comments) and renders colour, type, radius, spacing, layout, motion and hook tables.
- Editors reach surfaces only through a module's `brandVariant` field in Studio (`PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `BRAND_PRIMARY`, `INK`), which picks the band `Section` paints.
- `apps/platform` keeps its own `--platform-*` token layer (`apps/platform/src/styles/platform-theme.css`). It imports `theme.css` for the Tailwind base and for the site preview components.
- Platform tokens for the onboarding surfaces, in that file:
  - `--text-platform-28` (28px, `text-platform-28`): the wizard's step heading.
  - `--radius-platform-bar` (2px, `rounded-platform-bar`): progress segments and preset mini bars; the one 2px radius in the platform.
  - Six dark `rail` colours, as `platform-rail*` utilities: `--platform-rail` #0f1115 (the rail, the phone header and the Choose a workspace rail), `--platform-rail-line` #1c1f26 (rail borders and rules), `--platform-rail-text` #c9cfda (body text on the rail), `--platform-rail-muted` #7d8696 (upcoming steps, summaries), `--platform-rail-raised` #20242e (the current row, upcoming segments, hovers), `--platform-rail-accent` #ffc233 (done circles and segments, the current ring).
  - The `rail` colours are the same in Light and Dark and never follow the platform theme, unlike the sidebar's `side` tokens they were copied from. `tv()` lists `platform-28` and `platform-bar` so a later `text-platform-28` or `rounded-platform-bar` replaces an earlier platform size or radius.
- Platform selected tokens: `--platform-selected` and `--platform-selected-line` (#ffc233 in every theme), `--platform-on-selected` (#12141a Light, #0f1115 Dark), the tick on a small solid mark, and `--platform-selected-edge` (#12141a Light, #ffc233 Dark), the 1px edge that gives a picked control on `selected-tint` its 3:1 in both themes. `--platform-switch-thumb-on` is #12141a Light and #ffc233 Dark, so the on thumb shows on the tinted track. `--shadow-platform-choice-ring` is a tile's 1px `selected-edge` hairline.
- Platform hover tokens: `--platform-hover-tint` (#fff1c2 Light, #33290d Dark), the tint every pressable control and unselected option takes on hover; `--platform-bad-hover` (#fbdcd9 / #442020), the danger button's hover fill; and `--platform-control-line-hover` (#5b6472 / #5c6676), the off switch's hovered track. `--platform-selected-tint` (#ffe08a / #4a3b10) is the fill of every picked control, selected row and selected tile, a step stronger than the hover tint so the two never match. A test keeps the two tints apart.
- Platform focus tokens: `--platform-focus-ring` (#ffc233 in every theme) and `--platform-focus-edge` (#12141a in Light, `transparent` in Dark and system-dark). `--shadow-platform-focus-ring` and `--inset-shadow-platform-focus-ring` draw a 2px gap in `--platform-focus-ground`, the 2px ring and the 1px edge; `--shadow-platform-focus-glow` is the fields' 3px ring. `tv()` lists the shadows (and the inset one as an `inset-shadow`), so the inset ring stacks with a drop shadow and the outer one replaces an earlier platform shadow.

## Data

Nothing is stored; these are static CSS and TypeScript tables.

- **`@theme inline`** — semantic colour utilities (`bg-surface`, `text-text-muted`, `border-divider`, `ring-offset-ambient`, `bg-brand-primary-solid`, status `success`/`warn`/`error`/`info` and their `-muted` tints, `on-image`), `shadow-card`, `shadow-float`, and the font stacks. Each emits `var(--…)` rather than a literal.
- **`@theme`** — radius steps plus role radii (`--radius-card` → `--radius-md`, `--radius-media` → `--radius-lg`, `--radius-item` → `--item-radius`); the role hooks (below); containers; layout spacing (`gutter`, `section`, `page-y`, `site-x/y`, `card-x/y`, `card-gap`, and the `band` step with its `sm`–`xl` ladder); the type scale (`title-*`, `prose-*`, `card-*`, `meta`, `label`, `code`, …); tracking; easing.
- **`:root`, with `.dark` overrides** — page neutrals (`primary`, `primary-subtle`, `secondary`, `surface`, `surface-2`), borders (`border`, `border-strong`, `border-emphasis`), ink (`text`, `text-muted`), the brand ramp, `ring`, status colours, logo steps, shadows, durations, the on-image and scrim tokens, the seven `--item-*` card-style properties, the two header-control properties (`--header-control-border` → `--border-strong`, `--header-control-fill` → `--surface`, with no `.dark` copy), the navigation-link properties at their Plain values (`--nav-link-fill` and `--nav-link-outline: transparent`, `--nav-link-decoration: none`, `--nav-link-padding-x`/`-y: 0px`; `--nav-link-radius` is left unset so `rounded-nav-link` falls back to the nearest `--radius-sm`), and `--ambient`/`--ambient-raised` (→ `--primary-subtle`)/`--divider`/`--field-boundary`.
- **Palette tier** (`palette-ramps.ts`) — every colour `theme.css`'s `:root` and `.dark` hold, shadows included, as opaque stops in four families: `ACHROMATIC_PALETTE` (white, the near-white button label, black), `NEUTRAL_PALETTE` (hue 250), `BRAND_PALETTE` (no hue of its own, so it takes the tenant's) and `STATUS_PALETTE` (success, warn and error at their own hues; warn's dark-mode hue is its own `warn-yellow` family). Stops have fixed step names, `--palette-<family>-<step>`, numbered in hundreds from light to dark, so retuning a stop never renames it. A stop carries no alpha: a translucent role (the scrims, `--on-image-muted`) adds its own. The role ramps point at stops rather than restating values. `formatPaletteDeclarations(hue)` returns the palette's declarations at a hue. `formatSkinCss()` renders them at Console's hue on `:root`, `pnpm --filter @shinefold/config gen:skins` writes that to `configs/tailwind/skins.css`, and `theme.css` imports it; `skin-css.test.ts` fails while the checked-in file differs. On the site, `buildThemeStyleBlock` re-declares the `BRAND_PALETTE` stops on `:root` at the tenant's `accentHue`, so every skin that points at a brand stop follows the tenant's hue in both modes; the stops with a hue of their own stay in `skins.css` alone.
- **Skin tier** (`skins.ts`, `skin-roles.ts`, `skin-derive.ts`, `skin-contrast.ts`) — `SKIN_REGISTRY` (`satisfies Record<TBrandVariant, TSkin>`) holds one entry per band background, in Studio's order: Plain (`PRIMARY`), Shaded (`SECONDARY`), Brand tint (`BRAND_PRIMARY`) and Ink (`INK`). `defineSkin(title, { ground, mode, pin })` takes a palette ground per page mode and a `SKIN_MODE` — `MATCH` follows the page's light or dark, `INVERSE` is a dark band in both, deeper than the page in dark mode. It starts from the page's roles for the band's effective mode, sets `--ambient`/`--backdrop` and `--ambient-raised` to the ground and the scrims to the ground's family (brand scrim on a brand ground, neutral otherwise), applies the pins, then moves every unpinned role — ink and borders before fills — to the nearest stop in its family (by lightness, then chroma) that clears its contrast pairs at every accent hue; a fill (`--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--brand-primary-muted`, `--brand-primary-wash`) also never takes the ground's stop. If the result, pins included, still fails a pair or puts a fill on the ground, `defineSkin` throws, so the skin never ships. The roles are derived on first read of `roles`, since the search measures all 360 hues. A skin sets the ground and context (`--ambient`, `--backdrop`, `--ambient-raised`, `--divider`), ink (`--text`, `--text-muted`, `--text-subtle`), borders (`--border`, `--border-strong`, `--border-emphasis`, `--field-boundary`), fills, the brand ramp, the status colours and tints, the on-image scrims (`--scrim-strong/-mid/-weak`) and `color-scheme`, and re-declares every `TOKEN_ALIASES` entry that points at a role (`--ring`, `--info`, `--info-muted`, `--code-fg`, `--code-comment`) because a `var()` in a custom property resolves where it is declared; the generator and the contrast resolver read that one map. The skin check (`SKIN_CONTRAST_PAIRS`) is the page registry with `--primary` and `--secondary` read as the skin's ground, `--primary-subtle` as its `--ambient-raised` (so a photo-less Banner's copy is checked on it), the scrim tokens as the skin's scrims and `--field-boundary-tinted` as the skin's `--field-boundary`, less only the pairs the page itself fails; pairs whose surfaces no skin sets (header controls, navigation links, the logo tile) sit outside bands, and a pair mixing the two throws. `formatSkinCss()` adds a `[data-skin='X']` and a `.dark [data-skin='X']` scope per entry, each role as `var(--palette-…)`, and the `dark` custom variant, which also matches an inverse skin and its descendants. `STORYBOOK_SKINS` holds an inverse skin that never reaches Studio, the site or `skins.css`: `formatStorybookSkinCss()` writes its scopes, and a `dark` variant naming it, to `configs/tailwind/skins-storybook.css`. Plain resolves to the page's own roles in both modes, and pins `--ambient-raised` to `--primary-subtle`'s stop, the lifted field a photo-less Banner keeps; every other skin raises to its own ground. Shaded and Brand tint pin their divider to `--border-strong`'s stop and their field boundary to `--field-boundary-tinted`'s. The search moves Shaded's light `--border-emphasis` one stop darker and Brand tint's dark one one stop lighter, and Brand tint's `--brand-primary-muted` off its ground to the nearest stop that clears every pair, the wash stop in each mode. Ink is the one inverse registry skin: a neutral charcoal ground (`--palette-neutral-2100`) on a light page and the darkest neutral stop (`--palette-neutral-2700`, below the page's `--palette-neutral-2600`) on a dark one, with no pins, so every role is derived for its dark `color-scheme`. A footer may take it too (`footer_background` lists every `BRAND_VARIANT`), and the Look page's footer swatch for an inverse skin carries its `data-skin` so it paints the skin's ground.
- **Page defaults** — `theme.css`'s `:root` and `.dark` declare every skin role at Plain's value, including `--text-subtle` (an alias of `--text-muted` in both blocks, so a `.dark` wrapper below `:root` resolves it to the dark value; `text-text-subtle` and `text-subtle` read it) and the three `--scrim-*` roles (the neutral scrim, on `:root` only). `--field-boundary` is its own ramp token rather than an alias of `--border-emphasis`: `neutral-900` (0.62) in light, as before, and `neutral-1100` (0.58) in dark, one stop lighter than `--border-emphasis` so a field's outline clears 3:1 on `--brand-primary-muted` as well as on the page.
- **`@shinefold/config` theme tables** (`accent-ramps.ts`, `theme-ramps.ts`, `radius-declarations.ts`, `theme-declarations.ts`) — `ACCENT_RAMP_LIGHT/DARK` (with `--brand-primary-wash`, the lighter-in-light, darker-in-dark tint a Filled card takes on a Brand tint band), `LOGO_RAMP_LIGHT/DARK` (with `--logo-tile`, the fill of the initial tile a site with no logo shows — a dark tile in light, a pale one in dark — and `--logo-tile-contrast`, its letter, held to 4.5:1 by the contrast guard) and `IMAGE_SCRIM_RAMP` as OKLCH stops with fixed lightness and chroma, plus `RADIUS_DECLARATIONS`, `DENSITY_DECLARATIONS` and `CARD_STYLE_DECLARATIONS`. `formatOklchRamp` fills in the tenant's hue.
- **Colour maths** — `@shinefold/utils/color`: `renderedContrastRatio` (culori, OKLCH in, the worse of gamut mapping and clipping into sRGB), `oklchToHex`, `WCAG_AA_CONTRAST_MIN` (4.5).

## Configuration

- **Tenant (Look)** — the accent hue drives five of the six `--brand-primary*` tokens (`-contrast` is fixed) and the brand scrims. The logo hue drives `--logo-1..3`. The radius scale sets the five radius steps on a 4px base step — `--radius`/`--radius-md` 4, 8, 12 or 16px for Small to Extra Large, with `--radius-sm` at half, `--radius-lg` at 1.5× and `--radius-xl` at 2× the base (Medium is the static default), density the nine layout spacing tokens, and card style the seven `--item-*` properties. Neutrals, ink, status colours, the type scale, shadows and motion are fixed for every tenant. See [look](./look.md).
- **Dark mode** — a `.dark` class on `<html>`, set before hydration by an inline script from `localStorage.theme` or `prefers-color-scheme`. `@custom-variant dark`, generated into `skins.css`, matches `.dark` or any descendant, so a story can scope it to a wrapper, and also an inverse skin's scope and its descendants.
- No plan gating and no Voice reach.

## Constraints and invariants

- **Surfaces set, components read.** `--ambient` (default `var(--primary)`, read by `ring-offset-ambient`), `--divider` (default `var(--border)`, read by `border-divider`) and `--field-boundary` (default `neutral-900` in light, `neutral-1100` in dark, read by `border-field-boundary`/`bg-field-boundary`) are inherited. A skin scope sets them on every band; inside a band, two utilities retarget them for an item fill that reads the skin's own roles:
  - `surface-card`: ambient → `surface`, divider → `border`.
  - `surface-nested`: ambient → `surface-2`, divider → `border-strong`.
- **Brand tint follows the surface.** `bg-item-hover` and `bg-media-fill` read `--tint` (`--media-fill` for the icon tile), falling back to `brand-primary-muted` (`surface-2` for the tile). A skin on a brand ground (Brand tint) sets `--tint` to a 10% mix toward `brand-primary` (`formatSkinCss()` adds it to its scopes), so a hovered or focused card, a `MediaCard` icon tile and a linked `LogoTile` never take the band's own colour; Plain and Shaded bands, and the page ground, take the fallbacks. A `[data-skin]` scope unsets `--tint`, `--media-fill`, `--item-fill` and `--item-nested-fill` (through `:where()`, so it loses to a skin's own), so nothing around it leaks in. `surface-tint` points `--ambient` at the tint, with the band's divider and field boundary, for a card whose focus fill it is (`TaxonomyCard`). Menu rows fill with `bg-brand-primary-muted`.
- **Surfaces declare a skin.** `apps/web`'s `Section` (per `brandVariant`), `CtaModule` (the Split and Callout card, and the Banner), `Hero`'s Banner and `Footer` render `data-skin={tone}` and paint `bg-ambient`, so everything inside reads the skin's roles with no prop. The platform's Look preview draws one band per registry entry the same way (`look-sample-band/`), so every skin shows at the draft accent hue in both preview schemes. `Footer`'s `tone` defaults to `BRAND_PRIMARY`; on its `CARD` surface the card, not the full-width root, carries the skin. A Banner with a photo adds `skin-on-image`, which re-points `--text`, `--text-muted`, `--text-subtle`, `--border`, `--border-strong`, `--divider`, `--brand-primary` and `--brand-primary-hover` at the on-image colours; every Banner adds `skin-raised`, which points `--ambient`/`--backdrop` at `--ambient-raised`, so the lifted field shows wherever a photo does not cover it. Both are unlayered `theme.css` rules one class more specific than the skin scopes, since a layered utility cannot override a role on the element that sets `data-skin`. `Button`'s danger hover text reads `--brand-primary-contrast`, which `ERROR_FILL_TEXT` holds to 4.5:1 on `--error` on every skin. In `packages/ui`'s Storybook a Skin toolbar renders any story on every registry skin and on the Storybook-only inverse skin; `.storybook/preview.css` imports `skins-storybook.css` into the same Tailwind build, so its `dark` variant applies. `apps/web`'s `.storybook/preview.css` imports it the same way, for stories that place a component on the inverse skin.
- **Who applies them.** Every `bg-surface` card root (`Panel`, `PricingCard`, `Toast`, the `PopoverMenu` panel, item cards, `Accordion` items); and the `bg-surface-2` roots that hold focusable content (`MediaFrame`, `Aside`, `Panel.Header`, the compact `NewsletterSignup`). Image and scrim surfaces set neither.
- **`inline` is load-bearing.** A non-inline `@theme` would bake the `:root` value into the utility, and an ancestor's override would do nothing.
- **No `tone` prop, no context.** Everything stays server-renderable, and dark mode follows because the defaults are references.
- **Radius by role, never by step.** Bounded cards and tables use `rounded-card`, media frames `rounded-media`, and item cards the `item-card` utility's `--radius-item`. Button-like controls use `rounded-button`, header controls `rounded-header-control`; form fields (`FIELD_CONTROL`: TextInput, Textarea) take the radius scale's `rounded-sm`, so they follow Corners and never the Buttons shape; other controls and pills keep plain steps (`rounded-sm`, `rounded-full`).
- **Button shape.** `rounded-button` reads `--radius-button`, which `BUTTON_SHAPE_DECLARATIONS` sets to `0` (Square), `var(--radius-sm)` (Rounded) or `9999px` (Pill); every preset defaults to Rounded. The public site declares the stored `site_config.button_shape` under `:root`, beside the radius scale (a `FREE` site renders its preset's Rounded). It is a `theme.css` `@utility`, not an `@theme` token, and falls back to `var(--radius-sm)` at the element, so with no declaration it follows the nearest radius scale, the platform preview's frame included. Its readers: Button and LinkButton (a busy button's progress edge inherits the corners), Tag, StatusBadge, BookmarkToggle, the sign-in provider button, IconButton's default and `bordered` corners, SegmentedControl's root and options, PopoverMenu's `bordered` and `outlined` items, and Toast's action button. Form fields, header controls, IconButton's `circle`/`avatar`/`control`, avatars, text links, plain menu rows and the focus ring do not read it.
- **Navigation links.** `NAV_LINK` (`packages/ui/src/lib/styling/nav-link.ts`) styles first-level header links and the sub-menu trigger beside them, and nothing else: footer links, language codes, sub-menu items and phone-menu rows never read it. It applies `nav-link-frame` (a transparent 1px outline at rest, so a colour transition never fades one in from the text colour and hover changes only what the style marks), `rounded-nav-link` (`--nav-link-radius`) and `--nav-link-padding-x`/`-y`; the `nav-link-marked` utility (`--nav-link-fill` background and a 1px `--nav-link-outline`) on hover, on the current page and on an open sub-menu trigger; and `nav-link-current` (`--nav-link-decoration`) on hover and on the current page, so only Underline, whose decoration is not `none`, draws a line. `NAV_LINK_CURRENT` marks an active sub-menu trigger, which carries no `aria-current`. In forced colours `nav-link-marked` draws a `CanvasText` outline, since the fill and a transparent outline drop. The dropdown panel's anchor offsets subtract the link's padding, so it sits where it does for an unpadded trigger. `NAV_LINK_STYLE_DECLARATIONS` sets Plain (nothing — today's look, every preset's default), Underline (a 2px line under the current and the hovered link), Soft (a `--brand-primary-muted` fill at `--radius-md`) and Pill (a `--brand-primary` outline at `RADIUS_PILL`, shared with Pill buttons, and no fill). The public site declares the stored `site_config.nav_link_style` under `:root`, beside the radius scale (a `FREE` site renders its preset's Plain). The current link keeps its brand colour and weight under every style. The contrast guard checks `--text`, `--brand-primary` and `--brand-primary-hover` on `--nav-link-fill` under each style; `NAV_LINK_OUTLINE` holds Pill's outline to 3:1 against `--primary`, and a style whose outline is transparent is skipped.
- **Item cards.** `item-card` draws a solid border from `--item-border-width`, a left accent bar `--item-accent-width` wide, coloured `--brand-primary` mixed toward `--border` by `--item-accent-brand-share` (`100%` under `ACCENT_BAR`, `0%` otherwise) so the colour resolves at the card from the skin it sits in, a fill and `--item-shadow`. The fill mixes `--surface` toward `--item-fill` by `--item-fill-mix`. A band owns `--item-fill`, the tint a filled card takes on it, and `--item-nested-fill`, the fill of a card inside a card, each a single ramp token the contrast guard measures: the Brand tint skin sets `brand-primary-wash` / `surface`, Plain, Shaded and the page ground take the fallbacks `brand-primary-muted` / `brand-primary-wash`, and `surface-popover` unsets both. Card style sets only the `--item-fill-mix` switch, because a `var()` inside a custom property resolves where it is declared, so a colour declared on `:root` would ignore the band. The result is mixed toward `transparent` by `--item-clear-mix`, so `PLAIN` paints no background. An item card nested in another takes `--item-nested-fill`, so a filled card never matches its filled parent. `--item-divider-width` draws a top border on every accordion item and a bottom border on the last, at the wider of it and `--item-border-width`, so a style with no frame still draws lines above, between and below its items; `--item-stack-gap` and `--item-stack-radius` set the accordion's item gap and corners, both `0px` under `PLAIN` so its dividers stay straight. The bar is an `::after` over the left border's straight run, between the corners, so it stays straight on a rounded card. It is drawn as that `::after`'s left border rather than a fill, so forced colours keep it as a system-colour line, and a focused stretched link raises its ring above it. `ACCENT_BAR` (the static default) is borderless with a 2px brand bar; `OUTLINED` is a 1px hairline whose bar matches the border; `FILLED` has no outline and takes the band's tint (`--item-fill-mix: 100%`); `RAISED` is the hairline plus `--shadow-card`; `PLAIN` has no outline, fill or shadow and draws a 1px accordion divider. All round at `--radius-md`. `Accordion` items are item cards too, with the trigger and answer inside the `card-x`/`card-y` padding. `INTERACTIVE_ITEM_CARD` adds `bg-item-hover` on hover and focus-within: the band's tint, or under `FILLED`, which rests on a tint, the band's `--item-nested-fill` (`--item-card-hover-mix: 100%`, read only by `item-card`, so a `LogoTile` keeps the tint), plus a 1px `--border-strong` outline on hover and focus-within (`--item-card-hover-edge`) so it keeps an edge on every band. Under `PLAIN`, an item card's `--ambient` is the band's `--backdrop` (published by every skin beside `--ambient`) instead of `--surface`, so ring offsets inside a frameless card match the section; panels are unchanged. A card's quiet title link hovers to `brand-primary-hover`, which holds 4.5:1 on that tint in both schemes; `brand-primary` does not in dark.
- **Link underline.** Prose links, the `link` `Button` and a linked `QuoteCard.Name` draw one native `text-decoration` underline from `LINK_UNDERLINE` (`packages/ui/src/lib/styling/link-underline.ts`): a `0.2em` offset that clears descenders, 1px at rest and 2px on hover, with `text-decoration-skip-ink: auto`. `LINK_UNDERLINE_EMPHASIS` is the 2px weight on its own, for a current-page link. Callers set only the colour. Never a border or background image: those break on a wrapped link and vanish under forced colours.
- **Role hooks.** Five `@theme` tokens hold today's values so a later Look setting can change them per tenant without touching a component: `--font-weight-heading` (500, `font-heading`), `--heading-case` (`none`, the `heading-case` utility), `--aspect-card` (16 / 9, `aspect-card`), `--aspect-split` (4 / 3, `aspect-split`) and `--divider-width` (1px, read as `border-t-[length:var(--divider-width)]`). Each is a literal, so redeclaring it on any ancestor restyles what is inside. `Section`'s `dividerTop` and `dividerBottom` borders read `--divider-width`; no other line does. `Heading`'s base weight reads `font-heading` (`preview` keeps `font-semibold`), and only the page-level and section titles (`hero`, `post`, `page`, `section`) read `heading-case`; card titles and in-article headings keep their case so long text stays readable. Every `Heading` visual takes its line-height and letter-spacing from its own `--text-*` sub-properties; the card title pins `--text-card-title--line-height` so its container step-up to `text-lg` keeps it. The base `h1`–`h4` rule reads `--tracking-tight-display`. `tv()` lists `font-heading`, `heading-case`, `aspect-card` and `aspect-split` in their class groups, so each replaces an earlier weight, case or ratio.
- **Media proportions.** An image an editor left Wide, or one in a module with no shape choice, reads a ratio token; Square, Circle, portrait and `MediaFrame`'s own `ratio` variants stay fixed. `MediaCard`'s `wide` media reads `aspect-card`, and its lead `aspect-split`; `wideFlat` (2:1) is fixed. `CtaModule`'s Split media reads `aspect-split` from `sm` up and `aspect-card` below, where it stacks; the Hero's Split media at its default `video` ratio reads `aspect-split` from `lg` up and `aspect-card` below, and so does a `FeatureHighlights` row's frame, whose phone height cap still applies below `lg`. Embeds (`ConsentPlaceholder`), the article cover and Team's portraits keep their own ratios.
- **Elevation.** `shadow-card` is for raised cards (`Panel`, the highlighted `PricingCard`); `shadow-float` is for chrome above the page (`Toast`, `PopoverMenu`, `Dialog`).
- **Header controls.** `HEADER_CONTROL_FRAME` (`packages/ui/src/lib/styling/header-control.ts`) is a 1px frame whose corners are `rounded-header-control` and whose resting border and fill are `border-header-control` and `bg-header-control-fill`. `HEADER_CONTROL` adds ink text, a brand border and content on hover and while `aria-expanded`, a focus ring that leaves the fill unchanged, and a `Highlight` outline on hover and open in forced colours. IconButton's `circle` is built on it. Restyling every header control means setting the two properties, never changing a class.
- **Header-control shape.** `rounded-header-control` reads `--radius-header-control`, which `HEADER_CONTROL_SHAPE_DECLARATIONS` sets to `9999px` (Round), `var(--radius-md)` floored at Small (Rounded) or `0` (Square). Every preset's `headerControlShape` is Round, and the `theme.css` `@utility` falls back to `9999px` at the element, like `rounded-button`, so a site with no declaration keeps fully round controls. It reaches the language-menu trigger, the codes pill's frame and its current-language chip (`rounded-header-control-inner`: the frame's radius less the 3px gap, never below 0, so a pill, 5px inside an 8px Rounded frame, or square), the theme toggle, IconButton's `circle` (the menu button) and AccountTrigger in both styles; `header-control` sits in the `tv()` `rounded` class group so the token wins over IconButton's own corners. A signed-in avatar inside AccountTrigger stays a circle, and the floating cookie-settings button, which reuses `circle`, sets `rounded-full`. `site_config.header_control_shape` stores the shape and `buildThemeStyleBlock` declares it under `:root` (see [look](./look.md)).
- **Dividers.** Dividers on secondary and brand bands use `border-strong`, which is decorative and below 3:1 on purpose. `--border-emphasis` (3.29:1 light, 3.73:1 dark against `primary-subtle`) is the token for a border that is the only indicator.
- **Control boundaries.** An enabled `TextInput`'s border and an off `Switch`'s track use `field-boundary`, which reaches 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) against the `surface` field fill and against the band behind it on every brand variant, in both modes and at every accent hue: `--field-boundary` on page and card surfaces (`border-emphasis`'s stop in light, one stop lighter in dark), and the darker (light) or lighter (dark) `--field-boundary-tinted` on secondary and brand bands, where `border-emphasis` falls short. A disabled `TextInput` drops to `border-strong`, since inactive controls are exempt.
- **The accent and logo hues are the only tenant colour inputs.** Ramp lightness and chroma never vary, so a token pair's contrast depends on the hue alone. `isAccentHueAccessible` checks one pair (`--text` on `--brand-primary-muted`, both modes) and accepts every hue. Every other checked pair, the field boundary included, is registered in `CONTRAST_PAIRS` (`packages/config/src/theme/contrast-pairs.ts`) with its role minimum; `contrast-check.test.ts` measures each one on every surface it lists, in both modes and at every hue, and fails `pnpm verify` on any shortfall not recorded in `contrast-known-failures.ts`.
- **Focus under forced colours.** Components draw focus as a `ring` and drop the outline with `focus-visible:outline-none`; Windows High Contrast discards the ring's box-shadow. An unlayered `@media (forced-colors: active)` rule in `theme.css` restores a 2px `Highlight` outline on every `:focus-visible`, on the site and in the platform. It must stay outside `@layer`, since any layered rule loses to `@layer utilities`. Outside forced colours it does nothing.
- **Type scale in even pixels.** At a 16px root every fixed text size, and every fluid size's smallest and largest value, is an even number of pixels, and body and reading text (`text-base`, `text-prose`) is 16px. Fixed: `label`, `xs` and `meta` 12; `sm`, `copy`, `caption`, `code` and `card-copy` 14; `lead`, `card-title`, `base` and `prose` 16; `lg` 20. Fluid, smallest–largest: `prose-h4` 18–20, `prose-h3` 20–24, `xl` 22–26, `prose-h2` 24–30, `title-section` 26–32, `2xl`/`title-2xl` 28–36, `post-title` 30–40, `hero` 32–48, `3xl` 34–48, `4xl` 38–54, `display`/`wordmark` 42–74. A size change re-solves the clamp's middle term so it still spans the same viewport range. The platform's `text-platform-*` scale is separate.
- **`text-subtle` is `text-muted`.** The `text-subtle` and `subtle` utilities resolve to `--text-muted`; there is no separate dimmer ink, because no lighter grey reaches 4.5:1 on every surface. Size and font carry the lower step in the hierarchy.
- **Static defaults equal Console.** `theme.css`'s static values equal `buildThemeStyleBlock`'s Console output (`MD`, `DEFAULT`, `ACCENT_BAR`, hue 250), and a test reads `theme.css` for the radius, spacing, item-card and scrim tokens and `skins.css` for the brand palette stops, and checks the brand and logo ramps against fixed literals. Changing a static token means changing its declaration table too.
- The platform preview re-derives `--radius-card`, `--radius-media` and `--radius-item`, because `@theme` derives them on `:root`.
- Email cannot read CSS variables, so `resolveTenantEmailBrand` converts the light-mode ramps (`NEUTRAL_RAMP_LIGHT`, `ACCENT_RAMP_LIGHT`, `LOGO_RAMP_LIGHT`) to hex literals; a ramp change reaches tenant emails with no other edit.
- A consuming app adds its own tokens only for a dependency no other workspace uses (`apps/web/index.css`'s `--code-*` syntax colours, each a `light-dark()` pair so a skin whose `color-scheme` differs from the page takes the other mode's set; `--code-fg` and `--code-comment` follow the skin's text roles; the highlighted line's `--code-highlight` mixes `--surface-2` 10% toward `--brand-primary`, so it stays distinct from the block on every skin). The contrast guard measures the syntax colours on `--surface-2` and on that mix: `MIXED_SURFACES` in `contrast-surfaces.ts` holds its base, target and share, the guard composes it with `mixOklch` from `@shinefold/utils/color`, and a skin check counts a mix as a band surface when both its parts are skin roles. Shared tokens belong in `theme.css`.

- **Text roles.** `TEXT_ROLE` (`@shinefold/config`) names each kind of text — the titles (`HERO_TITLE`, `POST_TITLE`, `PAGE_TITLE`, `SECTION_TITLE`, `CARD_TITLE`, `ITEM_TITLE`, `DIALOG_TITLE`, `PROSE_H2`–`PROSE_H4`), `INTRO`, `BODY`, `CARD_BODY`, `META`, `EYEBROW`, `LABEL`, `FOOTNOTE`, `CAPTION`, `QUOTE`, `ATTRIBUTION`, `FIGURE` and `FIGURE_LABEL`. `TYPOGRAPHY` in `packages/ui/src/lib/styling/typography.ts` maps each role to its font, size, weight, leading, tracking and colour; `LABEL` carries no colour, because pills colour it by tone. `Text` takes `textRole` and renders in the element `as` names (`p` by default, or `span`, `div`, `dt`, `dd`, `li`, `figcaption`); `Heading`'s `visual` takes the title roles, and its keys `hero`, `post`, `page`, `section`, `card` and `prose-h2`–`prose-h4` read their matching title role. `Eyebrow` and `Caption` read the registry; `Caption` keeps its own 11px size until it moves onto the label token. `CARD_TITLE` steps up only inside a `@container/card`, and `FIGURE` steps from `text-2xl` to `text-3xl` and `text-4xl` only inside a container named `figures`. `Text`'s `variant` and `Heading`'s `preview`, `copy` and `size` stay until their callers move to roles. The design-tokens story's Text roles page renders every role in light and dark.
