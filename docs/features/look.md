# Look

## What it does

The Look page is where a tenant owner styles their public site without touching code. They pick a preset (Console or Editorial), then adjust the accent hue, the logo-mark hue, the heading and body fonts, corner radius, density, card style, the header buttons style, the header's wide-screen layout, sub-menu style and bar, the account button, account panel and where the account sits on phones, the busy-button style and spinner style, the footer's layout, background and surface, how readers reopen cookie settings (a footer link, a floating button or both), and the language-switcher style, and set a brand name and upload a logo and favicon. A live preview shows the result in a desktop, tablet or phone frame, in light or dark, and with the reader signed out or signed in before saving. One save restyles every page of the site, and the accent and logo hues also colour the tenant's emails.

## Surfaces and routes

- **Platform** — `/dashboard/look` (owner) and `/tenants/{id}/look` (operator). The edit column holds the Preset card, then a Style group (Colour, Brand, Type, Shape, Buttons) and a Site parts group (Header, Footer, Language switcher), each a section named by its heading that holds a card listing its folding rows (the shared accordion in its row variant, with `isList` so it renders as a `ul` of `li` rows), so a screen reader announces how many rows a group has and each row's position. Shape holds radius, density, card style and header buttons; Header holds layout, sub-menus, bar, Account button, Account panel and On phones, the account sits; Buttons holds While busy and Spinner, and hides Spinner while While busy is Progress edge (busy buttons draw no spinner in that style; the saved spinner is kept, still drives the site's other spinners, and shows again with another style); Footer holds Footer layout, Background, Surface and Cookie settings. Every row is folded on load, on every plan, and shows its description first when open. Opening a row folds the one that was open (several rows can be open once something opens them together); archived sites can still open and fold rows. A folded row stays mounted, so its draft values, descriptions and archived state are unchanged. A row with unsaved changes shows a dot named "Unsaved changes" beside its title. Inside an open row every field reads label, hint, then control, 6px apart, and clicking a label focuses its control without opening a select's list; a field whose value differs from the saved one shows a dot named "Unsaved change" beside its label (part of the field's accessible name), gone once the value is changed back. Footer layout is a segmented control like the other footer fields; Header Layout and Spinner are compact selects (at least 220px wide, 36px tall, at the start of the row), while the Type font pickers stay full width. Colour shows the accent as a 40px swatch beside the hue track and its "hue {n}°" readout; Logo hue shows only its "Follow accent hue" switch while it follows, and the three-tone swatch, slider and readout once switched off. Brand opens with a Brand name text field, its placeholder the tenant name; the preview's header shows the typed name as the owner types, and the tenant name while it is blank. A name over 60 characters (`BRAND_NAME_MAX_LENGTH`, after trimming) shows "Use 60 characters or fewer." under the field and blocks the save; a blank one saves as `null`. `tenants.name` stays the operator's account name and keeps its lock after provisioning. Brand then puts its formats line under the description and gives Logo and Favicon each a plain "optional" tag over an asset row (thumb, then Upload / Replace and Remove), with no staged-file badge — the field dot marks a staged image. Language switcher's style choice is labelled "Style", with options Code, Globe and Compact. Language switcher Style and Shape's Header buttons tiles show only their labels; one line below the control describes the selected option, changes with the selection, describes the group for assistive technology, and on a locked Free field describes the locked value (Code "The current language's code opens a menu", Globe "A globe icon opens the language menu", Compact "Language codes side by side, for a few languages"; Header buttons keeps its Outlined, Soft and Plain lines). Segmented controls whose options carry no description are unchanged; on a paid plan a one-language site shows only its note. A folded row shows a one-line summary of the current form values, unsaved edits and a staged logo or favicon included, joined by " · " and cut with an ellipsis; an open row shows none. Colour reads "Hue {accent}" then "logo follows accent" or "Logo hue {hue}", after the accent swatch (14px, 12px below `lg`, hidden from assistive technology); Brand reads "Logo uploaded" / "No logo" and "Favicon uploaded" / "No favicon"; Type the two font names; Shape radius, density, card style and header buttons; Buttons While busy and, unless it is Progress edge, Spinner; Header layout, bar, sub-menus and Account button (never the account panel or phone placement); Footer layout, background, surface and "Cookie link in footer" / "Floating cookie button" / "Cookie link and button"; Language switcher its style, then "One language" on a one-language site and on every `FREE` site. Every part but the fixed strings is the field's option label. Each open row body is a group named by the row title, so a screen reader announces the card before the field. The Account button hint points phone owners at the On phones, the account sits field. On `FREE` every field but the preset, accent hue, brand name, logo, favicon and Cookie settings is locked (see Configuration). Saving with an invalid field (a rejected accent hue, a brand name over 60 characters, a failed logo or favicon upload), from the Save button, ⌘S / Ctrl+S or "Save and leave", switches to Edit if Preview is showing, opens the row holding the first one, leaving the other rows as they were, and focuses it. The Preset card is not a row: a compact strip under its title and description, with one chip per preset (a glyph swatch and the preset name, the selected one bordered with a check) in a radio group named "Preset" that never wraps; arrow keys move and select, and a choice changes only the draft. Its title carries no unsaved dot. Below `lg` the chips are 44px and Reset to preset sits full width under them; from `lg` it sits at the right of the title row. The strip measures its width on resize and fits as many chips as it can in registry order, never shrinking or truncating one; the selected preset is always a chip, taking the last chip slot when it falls past the cut. When some do not fit, a More presets button (named "More presets, {n} more", with a count pill) follows the radio group, outside it, and opens a popover dialog under the strip as wide as the edit column: "Choose a preset" with a count of every preset over a two-column listbox. Focus starts on the selected preset; arrow keys move in both directions, Home and End jump to the ends, and Enter, Space or a click chooses, closes and focuses the chosen chip. Escape, Tab or a click outside closes with no change and returns focus to More presets. Below `lg` an overflowing strip becomes one 56px Preset button (swatch, name, description, "{n} presets", chevron), named by the card title and the preset, which opens a modal bottom sheet over the scrim with the same list as 64px rows; it closes on Close, Escape, a scrim click or a swipe down. On an archived site More presets and the Preset button are disabled and described by the archived notice. With today's two presets every preset is a chip at every width. Onboarding's `PresetPicker` cards are a separate component and unchanged.
- **Layout** — the page width is the content area. From 900px the edit and preview columns sit side by side (edit 460px, 500px from a 1120px content area; 24px gap; the preview takes the rest and sticks below the top bar and the jump index, always leaving 80px at the bottom for the save bar). Under 900px the Edit / Preview underline tabs, named "Look view", stick under the top bar, and the edit column fills the width. Below `lg` the page's visible h1 and subtitle are hidden (an `sr-only` h1 remains) and rows use the phone form (56px folded, 48px open); other settings pages keep their visible heading at every width.
- **Jump index** — a sticky `nav` named "Look sections" above both columns (hidden on the Preview tab): Preset, then the Style and Site parts group links, each followed by its card links. From `lg` it is a white bar ending in an Expand all button that reads Collapse all once every row is open and Expand all again as soon as one row folds; it sticks under the top bar in two columns and under the "Look view" tablist in the tabbed layout. Below `lg` it is a row of 44px chips on the page ground, sticky under the tabs, with the group names as plain labels and no Expand all. The current link (`aria-current="location"`) is the row last opened, focused or reached from the index while it stays open (focusing a folded row changes nothing; Preset, which never folds, hands back to the next rule); otherwise the topmost card in view below the index, following the page as it scrolls. A card link opens its row (folding the other open row), scrolls it clear of the top bar, tablist and index (the index adds its height to the page's scroll padding, so any focused control lands clear of it too), and focuses its row button; a group link scrolls to its group. A card with unsaved changes shows the dot and a hidden ", unsaved changes". When the links overflow, the list scrolls sideways with no scrollbar, edge fades show where more lies, and the current link is kept in view.
- **Public site** — no route of its own. `[tenant]/[locale]/layout.tsx` and the standalone not-found page wrap the page in `ThemeScope`, which hoists one `<style>` into `<head>` and sets the font variable classes. The header and footer read the language-switcher style. Every busy button and spinner on the site reads the busy-button style and spinner style. The header brand shows the Look logo and `app/icon.tsx` serves the Look favicon.
- **Email** — the platform's Email page names the brand name in its preview, falling back to the tenant name. `resolveTenantEmailBrand` turns the preset, accent hue and logo hue into hex colours for the magic-link, newsletter and test emails.
- No Studio surface: the theme is not Sanity content.

## Data

- `site_config` (Neon) — one row per tenant (unique `tenant_id`, cascade delete). Look columns: `preset` (`preset_id` pgEnum), `accent_hue` (int), `logo_hue` (int, nullable = follow the accent), `heading_font` and `body_font` (`font_choice`), `radius_scale` (`SM`/`MD`/`LG`/`XL`), `density` (`DEFAULT`/`COMPACT`), `card_style` (`ACCENT_BAR` default, `OUTLINED`), `header_control_style` (`OUTLINED` default, `SOFT`, `PLAIN`; a new row takes the preset's, `OUTLINED` for both; `HEADER_CONTROL_STYLE_DECLARATIONS` maps each to `--header-control-border` / `--header-control-fill`), `language_switcher_style` (`MENU_CODE` default, `MENU_GLOBE`, `CODES`), `account_button_style` (`PILL` default, `CIRCLE`), `account_panel_style` (`CARD` default, `PROFILE`, `HUB`), `account_phone_placement` (`BAR` default, `MENU`), `busy_button_style` (`LABEL` default, `KEPT`, `EDGE`), `spinner_style` (`BRAILLE` default, `RING`, `DOTS`, `PULSE`; a new row takes the preset's, Braille for Console and Ring for Editorial), `header_layout` (`CLASSIC` default, `LINKS_BESIDE_BRAND`, `CENTRED_LINKS`, `TWO_ROWS`, `CENTRED_BRAND`, `MENU_BUTTON`), `header_submenu_style` (`DROPDOWN` default, `WIDE_PANEL`), `header_bar_style` (`FULL_WIDTH` default, `FLOATING`), `footer_layout` (`COLUMNS` default, `CENTRED`, `COMPACT`, `BIG_WORDMARK`), `footer_background` (`BRAND_PRIMARY` default, `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY` — the `BRAND_VARIANT` skins), `footer_surface` (`FULL_WIDTH` default, `CARD`), `cookie_settings_button` (`HIDDEN` default — footer link only, `SHOWN` — link and floating button, `ONLY` — floating button only) — a new row takes the preset's, the defaults for both, and a preset change leaves them as they are; `brand_name` (text, nullable = the tenant name), `logo_asset_url`, `favicon_asset_url`. The same row holds Voice's `voice_overrides`.
- `PRESET_REGISTRY` (`@blog/config`) — `CONSOLE`: hue 250, Space Grotesk / Newsreader, `MD`, `DEFAULT`, `ACCENT_BAR`. `EDITORIAL`: hue 28, Fraunces / Inter, `SM`, `COMPACT`, `OUTLINED`. Each preset also carries the capability `featureDefaults`.
- Provisioning writes no row. With no row, the site renders the Console preset and the form opens on Console defaults.
- Brand images live in Vercel Blob at `tenants/<id>/<logo|favicon>-<random suffix>.<ext>`, public. Every upload gets a unique name, so a same-type replacement never collides with the old file or its cached copy. A replaced or cleared image's old blob is deleted best-effort.
- **Caching (web)** — `getSiteConfig` is `unstable_cache` per tenant, tag `site-config:<id>`, 3600s fallback (`TENANT_CONFIG_REVALIDATE_SECONDS`). `updateLookAction`, `uploadBrandAssetAction` and `clearBrandAssetAction` each POST `/api/revalidate-site-config` after their write, which expires the tenant's tags and purges the whole layout.

A locked field carries the shared `GrowthBadge` ("Growth", read as "Growth — included in the Growth plan") beside its label, and its control is in locked mode: `aria-disabled`, still focusable, keeping its value, ignoring activation (a select does not open), named by its label and the badge and described by the badge. Logo hue is the locked "Follow accent hue" switch, on, with the readout "follows accent" and no slider. A folded row whose every field is locked (Type, Shape, Buttons, Header, Language switcher) shows a Growth badge after its summary; Colour, Brand and Footer, which keep a live field, show none, and the group labels never do. - The five fonts are allowed in either role; each has a heading and a body loader in `apps/web`.
- On a paid plan the language-switcher control appears only when the tenant has more than one live language; on `FREE` it always shows, locked (or disabled on an archived site), with the Free message in place of the description.
- Cookie settings offers Footer link (`HIDDEN`), Floating button (`ONLY`) and Both (`SHOWN`), on every plan; there is no "neither". With the cookie banner off (`CAPABILITY.CONSENT_BANNER`, after plan clamping) the choice is disabled and described by a note that readers see neither while the banner is off; the stored choice is kept and applies again once the banner is on.

## Constraints and invariants

- The resolved theme is never partial. `toThemeTokens` fills every gap from the row's preset, and a missing row or failed fetch falls back to `CONSOLE`.
- Accent and logo ramps are declared in both `:root` and `.dark`. Radius, density, card style, header control style and the image-scrim ramp go in `:root` only (see [design-tokens-and-surfaces](./design-tokens-and-surfaces.md)).
- The public site reads the busy-button style and spinner style through `getBusyStyle` (`getSiteConfig`, falling back to the preset's, and to Console with no row); the spinner is the stored value, with no preset resolution. `SiteProviders` hands the pair to `BusyStyleProvider`, and every busy `Button` and every `Spinner` on the site reads it through `useBusyStyle`: the sign-in panel, the newsletter signup ("Subscribing…"), the contact form, the account page's Save, Unlink, Unsubscribe, Resend and Delete buttons, and the toast's loading spinner. A busy button only draws the style when it has a busy label, so each of these has a short fixed one. Linking a provider and the public unsubscribe page's plain form post have none and stay unstyled.
- A header buttons style changes only the resting border and fill. Outlined frames each control with `--border-strong` on `--surface`; Soft fills it with `--border` and no outline; Plain shows neither until hovered, so a Plain codes pill is carried by its brand-filled current chip. Geometry, hover, open, focus and forced colours are the same in every style, and every style combines with every language-switcher style. No style's resting cue reaches 3:1; each control is identified by its glyph or label, which WCAG 1.4.11 accepts.
- The public site resolves the stored header control style through `toThemeTokens` (falling back to the preset's) and `buildThemeStyleBlock` spreads `HEADER_CONTROL_STYLE_DECLARATIONS` into `:root`, so every header control follows it on the next render after a save (the existing `site-config:<id>` tag). Hover and open draw a brand outline in every style.
- `theme.css`'s static values equal what `buildThemeStyleBlock` emits for the Console preset; `build-theme-style-block.test.ts` reads `theme.css` for the radius, spacing, item-card, header-control and scrim tokens, and checks the brand and logo ramps against fixed literals.
- Accent-hue guard: the form blocks saving, taking the owner to the accent hue field, and `updateLookAction` refuses a hue that `isAccentHueAccessible` rejects. The site and emails replace such a stored hue with the preset's. The guard checks one pair, `--text` on `--brand-primary-muted` in both modes at 4.5:1, which every hue from 0 to 360 passes. So the refusal never fires today, and the other accent-dependent pairs (button labels, links on grey bands) are not checked.
- Plan: on Free only the preset, accent hue, brand images and Cookie settings are editable. `updateLookAction` refuses a save that changes any other field from both its stored value and the preset value Free shows, and writes nothing; the page names the refused fields and the current plan ([plans-and-entitlements](./plans-and-entitlements.md)). A Growth choice still as stored is kept, never dropped silently.
- The logo hue is unguarded; it colours only the generated three-layer brand mark.
- `ACCENT_BAR` item cards stay square whatever the radius; only `OUTLINED` follows the radius scale.
- Density rescales layout spacing only. Tailwind's base `--spacing` is untouched, so controls keep their size.
- Fonts are self-hosted woff2 files in both apps, with no Google Fonts fetch. Only the Console pair and the mono font preload on the site.
- Brand images: PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG. The logo may be up to 4 MB and 32–4000px; the favicon up to 1 MB, 16–512px and square. The server sniffs the real bytes and sanitises SVG; the client check is a convenience only.
- Images upload before the Look save. Each successful upload or clear revalidates the site itself, so an image already stored still reaches the public site when a later step of the save fails; a failed one does not revalidate.
- A failed image upload or clear stops the save: its message shows under that picker, which is marked invalid and takes focus, and the message shows in the page's error alert. Any other save failure shows the page's error alert too; the save bar keeps its unsaved state.
- While changes are unsaved and nothing needs attention, the save bar says where they are. One changed card is named by its row title in the page's language ("3 unsaved changes in Footer"), as a link that opens that row (folding the other open row, as opening any row does) and focuses its row button, which scrolls it into view. Two or more read "in N cards", muted, with no names. The cards come from each row's unsaved-dot state. Preset is never a card: a preset choice is counted through the Colour, Type and Shape cards it changes. `revealCard` in `use-look-open-rows.ts` is how a link elsewhere on the page opens and focuses a row.
- In the Edit / Preview tabs (content area under 900px) the save bar is hidden while Preview is selected and returns on Edit with the same changes; in two columns it always shows. Save, Discard, ⌘S / Ctrl+S and the leave-page guard act on every card whether or not the bar is hidden. A save refused by an invalid field while the bar is hidden returns to Edit and focuses that field, so the bar shows again.
- The public site's header logo is `logo_asset_url` and its favicon is `favicon_asset_url`, each falling back to the platform's default three-layer mark when unset. The Studio's Site Settings brand logo is not a source for either. A logo that fails to load in the browser, or a favicon the icon route cannot fetch within 5s, also falls back to the default mark, so a broken upload never breaks the page. The public site's CSP allows `https://*.blob.vercel-storage.com` in `img-src` so the uploaded logo loads; the favicon is served from the site's own origin. Both follow a save on the next render after `site-config:<id>` expires, subject to browser favicon caching.
- Every save writes an audit event (`SETTINGS_UPDATED` on `SITE_CONFIG`) whose details are the fields it wrote.
- `upsertSiteConfig` requires only the preset; any Look column a save omits is left as stored, or takes the preset's value on a new row.
