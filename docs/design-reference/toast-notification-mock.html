Static-first rendering

by Val · Aug 3 · 6 min · private — only you see it

Keeping the article prerendered while the engagement layer hydrates separately is exactly why the LCP stays flat here. The prose ships as one static file; only the interactive islands cost bytes — and the toast that confirms your save is one of them.

val@ovinnikov:~$ notify --demotoast

Fire a toast. Hover a live toast to pause its timer; click ✕ to dismiss early.

Anatomy — a compact terminal window: title bar echoes the command (bookmark · saved) with a type glyph and elapsed time; body shows the result line with a colored › prompt; optional action (undo ⌘Z); a 2px timer line drains left-to-right as the auto-dismiss counts down. Type colors map to tokens — --ok, --accent, --warn, --danger. Respects prefers-reduced-motion: no slide, no drain, still auto-dismisses.
