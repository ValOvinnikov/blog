## 1. Summary

This audit covered 21 page-builder modules, the 63 components in @blog/ui (the shared component library), the Look pipeline (the tenant-wide theme edited in apps/platform) and the M9 roadmap (M9 is the Portfolio milestone). It confirmed 126 of 136 findings, and 21 of those are high severity.

The foundations hold. Every module sits on one of three band tones, and every item surface already belongs to one of five families: ACCENT_BAR (a white card with a 2px brand bar on the left and square corners), OUTLINED_ROUNDED (rounded corners with a hairline border), FLAT (no surface), FILLED (a solid or tinted brand fill) and IMAGE_SCRIM (a full-bleed photo under a gradient).
The recipe inside each family drifts. The accent bar is drawn five ways (**F24**). Bounded cards round at three radius steps with no rule (**F64**). One family carries three interactivity contracts, so unlinked feature and team cards look clickable while linked logo tiles look dimmed (**F01**, **F02**, **F03**).
Editors meet the same drift in naming. "Brand Variant" means the band, the card fill or the scrim tint depending on the module (**F09**), and one alignment field has two titles (**F80**). The Look also reaches less than it promises. Its corner roundness skips the accent-bar cards, density skips section spacing, banner scrims ignore the accent hue, three of the five fonts offered per role fall back silently, and the preview leaves out roundness, density and the logo (**F10**, **F13**, **F14**, **F12**, **F16**).

The proposal keeps the five families, gives each one recipe built on Look tokens, and adds one affordance rule: an item may react to hover only when its whole surface is one link.
Each skin axis (an authored per-module control such as Background, Shape or alignment) gets one shared field in the Studio (the editing app), one editor title and one rule in SPEC.md. Stored keys stay. Only two things change stored data: Right alignment becomes Center on ten grid and list modules, and the call-to-action (CTA) module's layout becomes a registered type.
The Look gains a site-wide card style (Accent bar or Outlined), density takes over section spacing, and the preview shows what the site renders. The work ships as four epics of 22 PRs, one workspace each except where a type narrowing forces a combination, with two migrations that need a human to approve them.

Before any M9 module is built, the ticket-body corrections and the SPEC.md resync (PR 1) must land, so #3672 and #3771 stop citing helpers that do not exist. Next, **Q4** must settle what editors call Brand Variant. PR 2's shared fields then merge, because every new module copies them.

_How to read this document: sections 2 to 4 are the evidence, and sections 5 to 7 are the decision, covering the target design, the delivery plan and the open questions that need your answer._

## 3. How the Look reaches a module today

A tenant's Look passes through five steps, from a database row to a class name.

1. **Store.** Each tenant has one `site_config` row. It holds preset, accent hue, logo hue, heading font, body font, radius scale, density, language switcher style and two asset URLs (`packages/db/src/schema/site-config.ts:55-82`).
2. **Resolve.** `getThemeTokens` (called at `apps/web/src/app/[tenant]/[locale]/layout.tsx:72`) reads the row through a cached query (`apps/web/src/server/site-config/get-site-config/get-site-config.ts:13-35`). `toThemeTokens` then fills each null from `PRESET_REGISTRY`, using Console by default. If the accent hue fails the WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) contrast guard, it uses the preset's hue instead. Logo hue falls back to accent hue (`apps/web/src/utils/to-theme-tokens/to-theme-tokens.ts:28-47`, guard at :34-37, fallback at :41).
3. **Emit.** `buildThemeStyleBlock` writes one CSS string with a `:root` block and a `.dark` block (`apps/web/src/utils/build-theme-style-block/build-theme-style-block.ts:105-124`). `ThemeScope` hoists that string as a `<style precedence="theme">` element. It also wraps the page in the next/font variable classes (`apps/web/src/components/shared/theme-scope/theme-scope.tsx:16-33`).

## 5. Target design

The five lenses agree on direction. Where they disagree, this is what I chose and why:

- **Item radius.** The look lens put Panel, PricingCard and the item cards on one `--card-radius`. I split it into `--radius-card` for bounded surfaces and `--radius-item` for the house item card, so picking Accent bar never squares off a pricing card.
- **Card style values.** The look lens offered Accent bar, Outlined and Flat. I kept the editor lens's two values, because the visual pass found borderless cards already read as bare slabs on a Shaded band, and Flat items would lose their edge entirely.
- **Layout fields.** The editor lens collapses the five layout fields into Rhythm and Divider. I kept the stored object (skin lens) and moved the cost into density and a collapsed group, because Rhythm drops the None and Extra large values that live documents may hold, and it needs a migration on all 21 modules (left to **Q5**).
- **Alignment home.** The editor lens moves heading alignment and card alignment to the Look (the tenant-wide theme edited in apps/platform). I kept both per module (skin lens). The Studio seeds Left on every new module, so a site-wide default would only ever reach old unset values.
- **Stored keys.** The skin lens renames `cardAlignment` and the hero media-order keys. I unify helpers and titles only and keep `cardAlignment`, which #3245 already names. A key rename costs a gated production migration and changes nothing an editor sees.
- **CTA tones.** The editor lens folds the CTA's (call to action's) two tone fields into its Shape. I kept both and retitled them (skin lens). Folding them changes the stored shape and removes the card-on-band contrast that SPEC documents as deliberate (`SPEC.md:616-623`).
- **Timeline shape.** The editor lens folds marker style and orientation into one Shape. I kept them separate, because a three-value Shape drops combinations that are stored today.
- **Compact newsletter.** The surface lens allowed either ACCENT_BAR or outlined. I chose the outlined card step so both newsletter variants share one family, which the visual pass flagged.
- **Section rhythm token.** The lenses point Section at `--spacing-section`. I add a new `--spacing-band` instead. `--spacing-section` (3rem to 6rem) already pads the error pages through `py-section`, and it is about twice Section's MD step (2rem to 3rem, `section-variants.ts:17-30`), so reusing it would double every band.
- **Dark hairline.** The surface lens wants the dark-mode border bump on every outlined surface or on none. I chose none. The visual pass found the plain hairline legible on dark logo tiles, and LogoTile's is the only `dark:` border in @blog/ui.

### (d) The editor-facing model

**Once per site, on the Look page.** Pick a theme preset. Then adjust the accent colour, logo colour, heading and body fonts, corner roundness, spacing density and card style (Accent bar or Outlined). The preview shows every change in light and dark.

**Per section, in the Studio.** Write the content and choose a Background: Plain, Shaded or Brand tint. Where the section has one, choose its Shape (Split, Stacked or Banner on a hero; Callout, Split or Banner on a call to action) or its Display Mode (Grid or Carousel). Then choose Content Alignment: Left or Center, plus Right on heroes and calls to action. Card grids also offer Card Alignment.

**Only if needed.** A collapsed Spacing & Dividers group adds space above or below, changes the width and turns on divider lines. Left unset, the site's density decides.

**Never.** Corner radii, borders, shadows, hover effects, focus rings, divider colours and card padding all follow the Look. A card looks clickable only when it links somewhere.

## 7. Open questions for the product owner

1. **Should the Accent bar card round its corners with the Look's corner roundness?**
   - Recommendation: no. Accent bar stays square, and the Outlined card style carries the roundness. A 2px left border on a rounded corner bends into a hook at Large and Extra large.
   - What would change it: if most tenants are expected to keep the default Accent bar style, the roundness control stays inert on the most common item surface (**F10**). The bar should then round with `--radius-md`.
2. **Are static framed cards legitimate: PricingCard, the newsletter Panel, unlinked logo tiles and testimonial cards?**
   - Recommendation: yes. Restate `SPEC.md:980-985` as "a surface marks a bounded item; only the hover tint and the link overlay mark a click", and list the static consumers.
   - What would change it: evidence that visitors click static pricing or testimonial cards expecting a link. Those items would then drop to FLAT.
3. **One card style per site, or a per-module override?**
   - Recommendation: one per site, on the Look page, with no module field.
   - What would change it: a planned module, such as the gallery or a project list, that must look different from post cards on the same page.
4. **What should editors call "Brand Variant"?**
   - Recommendation: "Background", with the options Plain, Shaded and Brand tint. On the CTA, use "Card background" for the card fill and "Background" for the band behind it.
   - What would change it: a plan to make Brand primary a saturated brand fill. It renders as a pale tint today, per the visual pass. If it becomes a full fill, the label is "Brand".
5. **Do module presets (#2803) replace the per-module knobs?**
   - Recommendation: no. Presets pre-fill Background, Shape and copy, and the spacing, width and alignment fields move into a collapsed group. Nothing is deleted, so nothing needs a migration.
   - Checked 2026-10-07 against the development dataset (project `50l5r0vs`, 114 module documents): 26 of them store at least one layout value (spacing, width or divider). That is not "almost none", so collapsing the five layout fields is a migration, not a cleanup; the collapsed group stays.
   - What would change it: a decision that those 26 documents may be re-authored by hand. Then collapsing spacing to Tight, Normal and Roomy removes about half of all skin knobs (**F17**).
6. **Should Banner scrims follow the accent hue?**
   - Recommendation: yes for the Brand tint scrim, at today's fixed lightness and chroma. The neutral scrim stays hue-free. On-image copy moves to tokens either way (**F14**).
   - What would change it: a contrast measurement of white copy over the Right and Center scrims at the extreme hues the accent guard accepts. One WCAG AA failure keeps hue 250.
7. **May grid, list and carousel modules drop Right alignment?**
   - Recommendation: yes. Ten modules keep Left and Center, heroes and the CTA keep Right, and stored Right values migrate to Center (**F42**).
   - Checked 2026-10-07 against the development dataset: eight documents store Right, one each on hero-profile, feature-highlights, newsletter, post-latest, post-list, post-related, stats and taxonomy-list. The hero keeps Right; the other seven would migrate to Center. Production stores none.
   - What would change it: any of those seven being a deliberate layout rather than a test page. Then that module keeps Right, documented in SPEC.
8. **Should every font be offered for both headings and body?**
   - Recommendation: yes, by adding a loader per role in `apps/web/src/config/fonts.ts`. The pickers already offer all five, and narrowing them would strand any saved row that holds a hidden value (**F12**).
   - What would change it: a font that does not work in a role, for example JetBrains Mono as body copy. That role's picker then narrows instead.
9. **May density change the band rhythm of existing sites?**
   - Recommendation: yes, but ship PR 14 with the DEFAULT step equal to today's MD and the COMPACT step one notch tighter, and say so in the PR body. Every Editorial-preset tenant ships COMPACT today, so their section spacing tightens on merge.
   - What would change it: a tenant on Editorial who must not change; then COMPACT keeps today's rhythm and only gutters and card padding stay compact.
10. **When a module's alignment is unset, does a Stacked hero or a Callout CTA fall back to Left or to Center?**

- Recommendation: Left, matching the Studio seed, so a document and its rendering agree. Older documents that relied on the Center fallback are re-saved with Center by the PR 4 migration's dry-run report.
- Checked 2026-10-07 against the development dataset: 25 documents leave alignment unset (16 post-list, 3 content, 2 newsletter, 2 taxonomy-list, 1 post-related, and 1 module_content on production); none is a hero or a CTA, so no live Stacked hero or Callout CTA relies on the Center fallback today.
- What would change it: a tenant site authored before this lands with an unset hero or CTA alignment. The PR 4 migration dry-run lists them.

11. **Is legacy `module_hero` live on production?**

- Checked 2026-10-07 against the production dataset (project `7fuqzuyl`): it holds three module documents in total, one of which is a `module_hero`, beside one `module_content` and one `module_postList`. No module there stores Right alignment, and all three leave alignment unset.
- Recommendation: retire it first through #2813 (one document to migrate onto `module_heroBlog`), so PR 3d never touches `module_hero`.
- What would change it: a decision to keep the legacy hero past this programme. Then PR 3d pins its `BRAND_PRIMARY` initial value as written above.

12. **Does dropping the embed module's `size` field change the design of record for #2788?**

- Recommendation: yes, and the issue-body pass in PR 1 says so in #3771 and the epic. Section 5(e) makes NARROW a Section width, so a per-embed size axis has nothing left to control.
- What would change it: a planned embed that must be narrower than the reading measure while the section stays wide.
