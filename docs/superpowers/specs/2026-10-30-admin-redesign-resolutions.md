# Cross-set resolutions: SH1 · TH1 · Dashboard LB/DB3 · Look LX/LXG/G2 · Voice V3a · Onboarding W2

Inputs: the six specs in this folder, the tickets of the six sets on the board and the 22 boards on the canvas.

## 0. The rule as applied

NEW-look-25 and NEW-look-mt2 are Look tickets not yet filed; they are named by their draft ids wherever they appear. Every other `NEW-*` id is a draft that was dropped or merged (§2, §3).

Two user decisions of 30 Oct set the rule below:

1. **The shared preview.** Look and onboarding use one device-frame preview. Onboarding shows the chosen site address in a readout above the frame, with no reader switch and no follow control.
2. **The precedence rule.** SH1 owns shell structure and TH1 owns colours and tokens. Layout B owns cards, status pills, the page heading, detail lists and the tooltip look. LX + G2, V3a and W2 own their own pages, with the shared preview. Pages drawn only as a backdrop are out of scope, and frames no board drew are drawn before filing.

Every split decision in this set was approved on 31 Oct.
